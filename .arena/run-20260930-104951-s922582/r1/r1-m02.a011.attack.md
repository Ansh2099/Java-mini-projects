ATTACK 1 [FATAL] The top Europe pick is already a built-in feature of the ERP it names as its "strong target"
Where: Software table: "Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central | UK, NL, Nordic medium firms | Yes | Good REST API. Strong target." Europe workflow 1 f, gap 1: "Many are built for US ERPs ... A 60 person UK distributor on Business Central, Exact or Unleashed is not their priority." 90 day plan step 3: "contact UK and Dutch distributors on Business Central, Exact or Unleashed."
Problem: The task says "If it is already solved by a built in feature or a cheap tool, reject it." Business Central ships a built-in Sales Order Agent that watches a shared Microsoft 365 mailbox, reads the email body and PDF or image attachments, matches the customer by email, checks availability, drafts a sales quote and can convert it straight into a sales order, with a human reviewing each step (Microsoft Learn: https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/sales-order-agent and https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/release-plan/2025wave1/smb/dynamics365-business-central/capture-more-sales-enhanced-sales-order-agent, which also adds Denmark, Germany, Italy, France and Spain). That is exactly "customer PO email to draft sales order, human approves" inside the ERP the answer tells the user to chase first. The answer never checked for it. Its other gap claim ("built for US ERPs") is also wrong for the very vendor it quotes for the EUR 12 to 28 per order figure: Graip.AI is a Latvian company that creates sales orders in SAP, Oracle and Dynamics and markets a European distributor case study (https://graip.ai/wholesale-distribution, https://rigacomm.com/en/meet-graip-ai-born-in-latvia-built-for-the-world/). Result: the user's first European sales calls, as scripted, land on buyers whose ERP already does the job, and the "gap" pitch collapses. The pick needs to be re-scoped to ERPs without such a feature, and the answer gives no such check.

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] India pick breaks on its own intake design: forwarded WhatsApp messages lose the dealer's identity
Where: India workflow 1, edge cases: "Smallest safe start: order staff forward order chats to one dedicated number or email that we read. The dealer changes nothing." Step 3 of today's process: "check the dealer's rate and scheme." Build plan: "Draft orders for the top 20 dealers."
Problem: When a WhatsApp message is forwarded, the receiving number sees it labelled "Forwarded" with no original sender. So our system gets "20 box Lux 100 wala, 5 Dettol 500" with no idea which dealer sent it, which means it cannot pick the dealer ledger, rate, scheme, credit limit or the per-dealer nickname table the answer depends on. Staff would have to type the dealer name with each forward, which is a new manual step on every order and exactly the "change how staff work" the task forbids ("We do NOT want processes that are fully manual today, because then we would have to convince both the decision maker and the staff to change how they work"). On top of that, version 1 sends photos and voice notes to exceptions, while step 1 lists them as a normal way dealers order. So the v1 that "works" handles only typed, forwarded, manually tagged orders. The answer gives no share of orders that fall in that bucket and no fix for the lost sender.

ATTACK 6 [MINOR] "Human approves in Tally" is never explained, and Tally has no draft or approval state for a pushed sales order
Where: India workflow 1 build step 2: "Draft orders for the top 20 dealers into Tally through the local agent. Human approves in Tally."
Problem: A Sales Order voucher imported through Tally's XML interface is saved as a normal voucher; it shows in order outstanding reports at once.
