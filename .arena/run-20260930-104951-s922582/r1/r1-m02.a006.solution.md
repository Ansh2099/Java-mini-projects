# Workflows Worth Selling: India and Europe (30 September 2026)

## The short answer

1. **Build one thing first: "customer order arrives, draft sales order is ready to approve."** Orders come in by email (Europe) or WhatsApp and email (India). Today a person reads each one and types it into the ERP or Tally. We read it, match every line to the right product code, show a draft for approval, and flag anything odd. A human clicks approve. This passed every filter in both regions, and an Indian distributor gives you a real case study for a UK wholesaler.
2. **But in Europe, check the client's ERP first.** Some ERPs now do this job themselves. Microsoft Business Central has a built in Sales Order Agent that is paid per use and costs pennies per order. Cin7 has built in PDF order capture. Odoo has cheap app store modules. **Do not pitch order entry to those clients.** Your order entry targets are firms on **Sage 200, Exact Online and Unleashed**.
3. **Second product for Europe: "supplier order confirmation arrives, checked against our purchase order."** No built in feature or cheap tool does it for SMB ERPs that I could find. It is the product you pitch to Business Central users, and the upsell to your order entry clients.
4. **Do not sell supplier invoice entry, remittance matching, Tally purchase bill entry, or freight quoting.** Cheap tools or crowded markets. Details below.
5. **Your Europe price guess holds, with a volume rule.** Build: 1,500 to 4,000 USD is right. Monthly: 400 to 1,000 USD for normal SMB volume, and 1,000 to 1,500 USD only above about 2,500 orders a month. Do not sell below about 500 orders a month. One price table below covers every case.
6. **Hard truth:** order entry automation is a busy market in 2026. Funded vendors, UK dev shops and ERP makers all sell it. You will not win on features. You win on price, hands on setup, and ERPs the big vendors skip. If you cannot get 5 real sales calls with distributors in your first 60 days, stop and rethink.

## Assumptions

1. "Europe" means the UK first, then the Netherlands, Ireland and the Nordics, because English works for sales there. Germany and France are harder to sell to from India.
2. "Few weeks of part time work" means about 60 to 100 hours of build time for two people.
3. "Under 50 USD a month" covers our own tools only. Each client pays its own hosting, AI and WhatsApp usage inside the monthly fee.
4. Where I give time per task without a source, I say it is an estimate. Every workflow includes a way to count it at the client before you quote.
5. How I got the facts: I used web search and cite only links it returned. Most sites blocked direct page opening from my tools, so many figures come from the search result text for that link. Vendor numbers (like "85% faster") are the vendors' own claims. Treat them as marketing, not proof.
6. Exchange rates used: 1 GBP = 1.32 USD and 1 EUR = 1.13 USD, the 30 September 2026 rates ([Trading Economics GBP](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-kingdom/currency), [Trading Economics EUR](https://tradingeconomics.com/euro-area/currency)).

## Step 1: The box we have to fit in

I started from your limits, not from ideas. The tightest limit decides everything.

| Limit | What it forces |
|---|---|
| 2 part time developers | One client build at a time. Nothing that needs 24/7 support. |
| Under 50 USD a month | Self hosted n8n or a small Spring Boot service on one cheap server. No paid platform seats. Xero's free Starter API tier allows only 5 connected organisations ([Truto](https://truto.one/blog/xero-api-pricing-changes-2026-costs-tiers-and-how-to-minimize-egress/), [Xero Developer](https://developer.xero.com/pricing)). |
| India time zone, Europe clients | UK 9:00 is 13:30 or 14:30 in Ludhiana. Fine for meetings. But if our system breaks at 16:00 UK time, you are asleep. **So the business must keep working when our system is down.** |
| Human approves, we never move money | Output is a draft shown on an approval screen. Nothing becomes a real order until a person approves it. No payments. |
| No legal, medical, lending decisions | Rules out insurance claims, clinic work, KYC approval, visa work. |
| 2 to 3 tools, one owner | Inbox or WhatsApp, plus the ERP, plus our approval screen. Nothing that crosses departments. |
| Already partly digital | Data must already arrive as email, PDF, Excel or WhatsApp. Nobody changes how they send it, and staff do not get a new manual step. |
| Not already solved | If the client's own ERP or a cheap add on does it, walk away. This must be checked per client, not per market, because it depends on their ERP. |
| Sold from India to Europe | Buyers will ask "what happens to our data" and "who else have you done this for". We need GDPR answers and a case study. |

**The limit that drives the design is the time zone plus "never in the critical path".** The original email or WhatsApp message still reaches the human exactly as today. We only add a draft next to it. If our server dies, the staff member types the order like last week. Nothing is lost. This rule kills live chat bots, payroll deadlines, and anything with a same day cut off that only we can do.

## Step 2: The edge cases that kill these projects

I hunted the failure modes first, then picked workflows that survive them.

| Failure mode | Real example | How the design survives it |
|---|---|---|
| **ERP already does it** | Business Central's Sales Order Agent reads a shared mailbox, finds the customer, drafts a quote for a human to review, and turns it into a sales order. It is generally available in all countries, billed in Copilot Credits at 0.01 USD each, and Microsoft's example flow uses about 7 credits ([Microsoft Learn: Sales Order Agent](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/sales-order-agent), [Microsoft Learn: consumption billing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/administration/tenant-admin-center-manage-consumption-billing), [MSDynamicsWorld](https://msdynamicsworld.com/blog-post/business-centrals-ai-agents-run-consumption-billing-heres-what-means-your-2027-budget)). Cin7 Core's IDR turns PDF POs into draft sales orders ([Cin7 help](https://help.core.cin7.com/hc/en-us/articles/10553586503439-Extract-sale-data-with-Intelligent-Document-Recognition-IDR)). | Ask on the first call: "Which ERP and version? Have you tried its AI order feature?" If it has one, pitch the supplier confirmation workflow instead. |
| ERP has no usable API | Sage 50 is a desktop product. Its free Sdata API covers only part of the features ([Sage Community](https://communityhub.sage.com/gb/sage-50-accounts/f/announcements/269682/sage-50-cloud-accounts---frequently-asked-questions---third-party-integration-and-apps), [Sage Developer Community](https://developer-community.sage.com/topic/495-api-for-sage-50/)). | No API, no deal. Or quote extra for an on site connector like HyperAccounts ([Hyperext](https://www.hyperext.com/sage-50-rest-apis/sage-50-accounts-api/)). |
| Software sits on one office PC | Tally only listens on a local port (default 9000) on the machine running it ([TallyHelp](https://help.tallysolutions.com/xml-integration/)). | Never open that port to the internet. A tiny agent on the client PC *pulls* approved orders from our server and posts them to Tally locally. If the PC is off, orders wait in a queue. |
| **Forwarded WhatsApp loses the sender** | A forwarded message shows "Forwarded" with no original sender, so we cannot tell which dealer sent it. | Do not use forwarding. Use WhatsApp coexistence (details in India workflow 1): each inbound message reaches us with the sender's number. |
| AI misreads a number | "10" read as "100". A 5 line PDF becomes 4 lines. | Hard checks: line totals must add up to the order total; quantity is compared with that customer's past orders; unknown product code goes to exceptions. Any failed check means a human looks. |
| Customer uses their own product names | "Blue widget 5L" or "Lux 100 wala" instead of your SKU. | A mapping table per customer that learns from every human correction. Week 1 has more exceptions. That is expected and written into the contract. |
| Same order sent twice | Customer resends "in case you missed it". | Duplicate check on customer PO number plus lines plus date. |
| Order changes and cancellations | "Please change PO 4471 qty to 20." | Version 1 does **not** apply changes. It flags them as "amendment, human action". |
| Low volume | 200 orders a month. | Below about 500 orders a month in Europe the saving does not cover a 400 USD fee (math in the Europe price section). Do not sell. |
| IT blocks mailbox access | Microsoft 365 admin must approve API access. | Fallback: client sets a forwarding rule to our intake address. For email this is safe, because the forwarded email keeps the original sender in the headers or the quoted "From" line. |
| Personal data and GDPR | EU buyers ask where data goes. | B2B orders carry little personal data (a buyer's name and email). Host in an EU AWS region, sign a DPA, keep documents 90 days. |
| WhatsApp rules change | One 2026 India pricing guide says charges for service messages inside the 24 hour window start from 1 October 2026 ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/india/)). | We mostly *receive* messages. Price any WhatsApp and provider fees as pass through cost, not inside a fixed fee. Verify before quoting. |

## Step 3: How I filtered ideas

Every idea had to pass all of these, in order. It dies at the first "no".

1. Does a named role do it by hand today, with the data already digital?
2. Can we count it (how many, how long)?
3. Only 2 to 3 tools, and do they have APIs we can reach?
4. Does a cheap tool or built in feature already do it *for this client's software*? If yes, reject.
5. Can the business keep running if we are down?
6. Can we build a working first version in 60 to 100 hours?
7. Will they pay more than it costs us to support, and does it save them more than we charge?

## Part 1: India

### Price reality in India

A Tally data entry person costs about ₹20,000 to ₹30,000 a month ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/india-data-entry-tally-salary-SRCH_IL.0,5_IN115_KO6,22.htm)). The average data entry clerk earns about ₹22,249 a month ([Indeed India](https://in.indeed.com/career/data-entry-clerk/salaries)). At about 200 working hours a month, that is roughly ₹2 per minute. So an automation that costs ₹15,000 a month will lose to "hire one more boy". It only wins when:

1. Volume is high enough that it replaces one or more people, or
2. Speed matters (orders at 7 pm must be packed by 9 am), or
3. Errors are costly (wrong item shipped, wrong rate given to a dealer).

### India workflow 1 (strong): Dealer order on WhatsApp or email to approved sales order in Tally

**a. Who has it.** Distributors and wholesalers: FMCG, pharma trade distribution (trade only, no medical decisions), electrical, hardware, auto parts, cycle parts, hosiery and textile wholesale (Ludhiana has plenty). Fits **medium** best (50 to 250 staff, often 1 to 3 order entry people). **Small** firms with 100+ orders a day also fit. Small firms with 20 orders a day do not; a clerk is cheaper.

**b. Steps today.**
1. Dealer or salesman sends an order on WhatsApp: typed list, Hinglish text, photo of a handwritten list, sometimes a voice note. Bigger buyers email a PDF or Excel.
2. One person at the distributor reads every message and types it into Tally or the ERP. Vendor articles on WhatsApp ordering describe exactly this ([Fast2SMS](https://www.fast2sms.com/help/tally-whatsapp-for-distributors/), [Rupyz](https://rupyz.com/blog/order-management-system-with-whatsapp-tally-integration/)).
3. They look up the item code, check the dealer's rate and scheme, and create the sales order.
4. Godown picks from the order. Invoice follows.

**c. Software.** In: WhatsApp Business app on a phone, Gmail. Out: TallyPrime mostly, also Busy and Marg. Tally accepts vouchers through its XML over HTTP interface ([TallyHelp](https://help.tallysolutions.com/integrate-with-tallyprime/), [community docs](https://noumaanahamed.github.io/tally-prime-api-docs/)). Busy and Marg: I did not confirm a usable API. **Start with Tally clients only.** In the middle: our approval screen.

**d. How often, how long.** Medium distributors get dozens to hundreds of orders a day. Vendor articles claim mid market firms run manual processing teams of 10 to 15 people with 12 to 18% error rates ([Hublerx](https://www.hublerx.ai/resources/blog/top-whatsapp-order-management-software-manufacturers-india-2026), [Wydely](https://www.wydely.io/whatsapp-automation-wholesale-trading-businesses.html)). Treat that as marketing. My estimate: 3 to 10 minutes per order, more for photos and long lists. **Count it at the client:** coexistence history sync (below) pulls up to 6 months of 1:1 chats, so you can count orders and their type before you quote, and match them against Tally sales orders.

**e. Who does it and what it costs.** Order entry or billing staff at ₹20,000 to ₹30,000 a month each ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Job/india-tally-operator-and-data-entry-jobs-SRCH_IL.0,5_IN115_KO6,35.htm)). Example: 100 orders a day at 5 minutes each is about 8 hours a day, which is one full person. The hidden cost is the wrong item shipped and the late evening backlog.

**f. Existing tools and the gap.**
* Order apps like Rupyz sync orders to Tally ([Rupyz](https://rupyz.com/blog/order-management-system-with-whatsapp-tally-integration/)), and many WhatsApp tools push catalog orders ([Wydely](https://www.wydely.io/whatsapp-automation-wholesale-trading-businesses.html), [Sunray](https://sunraydatalinks.com/blog/whatsapp-order-management-system-for-distributors-wholesalers/)). **The gap:** most make the dealer order through an app, a catalog, or a fixed format. Dealers refuse. That is exactly the "change how people work" problem you want to avoid.
* Tools like Fast2SMS send invoices *out* of Tally on WhatsApp ([Fast2SMS](https://www.fast2sms.com/help/tally-whatsapp-automation-auto-send/)). That is the other direction.
* **Honest risk:** AI players like Hublerx are now aiming at free text WhatsApp orders. The gap is closing. Treat India as your case study engine, not your long term business.

**g. Realistic price.** Setup ₹30,000 to ₹75,000. Monthly ₹5,000 to ₹12,000 plus WhatsApp provider and AI usage at cost. For your first 1 or 2 clients, charge only setup at cost in exchange for a named case study, real numbers, and a video testimonial.

**h. Sources.** Linked inline above and in the source list.

**How we get the WhatsApp orders without losing the dealer's name.**
* **Do not ask staff to forward chats.** A forwarded message arrives with no sender, so we cannot pick the dealer's rate, scheme or nickname table. And tagging each forward by hand is a new manual step.
* **Use WhatsApp coexistence.** The distributor keeps their number on the WhatsApp Business app, and the same number is also connected to the Cloud API. Staff keep chatting on the phone as today. We get every inbound 1:1 message with the sender's phone number. Coexistence is available for Indian numbers ([MSG91](https://msg91.com/guide/unlock-whatsapp-coexistence-use-whatsapp-business-app-and-cloud-api-together), [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/coexistence-for-smbs/)). In India, API use under coexistence is mostly limited to incoming messages, which is all we need.
* **Bonus:** on setup it can sync up to 6 months of 1:1 chat history ([360dialog docs](https://docs.360dialog.com/docs/resources/phone-numbers/coexistence), [InstantReply](https://www.instantreply.co/blog/whatsapp-coexistence-what-actually-syncs-2026)). That gives us the dealer nickname table and the order count on day 1.
* **Limits you must check on the first visit:** group chats, broadcast lists, view once and disappearing messages do not sync, and the phone app must be opened at least every 14 days ([360dialog docs](https://docs.360dialog.com/docs/resources/phone-numbers/coexistence), [seven.io](https://help.seven.io/en/whatsapp/whatsapp-coexistence)). If salesmen post orders in a WhatsApp group, those orders are invisible to us.
* **Setup:** coexistence is switched on through a WhatsApp provider (BSP) such as 360dialog. Its fee is passed to the client.
* **If the owner will not turn on coexistence:** version 1 covers email orders only, or you walk away. Do not fall back to manual forwarding.

**Go or no go gate, from the synced history.** Count the last 3 months of orders by type: typed text, PDF or Excel, photo, voice note, and orders in groups. Sign only if at least 70% of orders arrive in 1:1 chats or email as text, PDF, Excel or photo. If voice notes or group orders are most of the volume, v1 will not move the needle. Say no or wait.

**Other edge cases in India.**
* **Photos.** Current vision models read printed and many handwritten lists. Version 1 drafts photo orders too, but marks every photo draft "needs review" and never skips the approval. Voice notes come in version 3.
* **Product nicknames.** Build the nickname table from the last 3 months of Tally sales plus the synced chats before go live.
* **Schemes and credit limits.** We never approve credit. We only flag "dealer over limit" if Tally shows it.

**Where the human approves, exactly.** On our own approval screen, not in Tally. Developer B builds a simple web page (works on a phone) that shows the original message or photo on the left and the draft order on the right, with the exceptions in red. The order staff member edits and clicks approve. Only then does the local agent post the Sales Order voucher to Tally. So a wrong AI draft never shows up in Tally's order outstanding or stock reports. If an owner insists on reviewing inside Tally, the agent can post the order as an **Optional** sales order, which Tally supports for sales orders ([TallyHelp: sales orders](https://help.tallysolutions.com/sales-order-tally/)), and staff make it regular after checking. Test that on the client's Tally version before relying on it.

**Smallest version that works (build in this order, keep it working at each step).**
1. Shadow mode, 2 weeks: coexistence on, read orders, write the parsed order to the approval screen only. Staff keep typing into Tally as normal. Compare line by line. This gives you the accuracy number for the case study with zero risk.
2. Approve and post for the top 20 dealers: approved orders flow into Tally through the local agent.
3. All dealers in 1:1 chats and email, plus the exceptions list (unknown item, rate mismatch, duplicate, over limit).
4. Voice notes, then amendments.

### India workflow 2 (conditional): Freight rate request to draft quote, for small forwarders

**a. Who.** Freight forwarders and customs brokers serving exporters (Ludhiana exporters use ICDs and Delhi forwarders). **Small** mostly.

**b. Steps today.** Exporter emails or WhatsApps "rate for 1x40HC Ludhiana to Hamburg". Staff read it, check carrier rate sheets in Excel or ask agents, add margin, and type a quote email.

**c. Software.** Email, WhatsApp, Excel rate sheets. Some use a freight system.

**d. How often, how long.** Vendors claim 2 to 4 hours per request, cut by 85% ([41 Labs](https://41labs.ai/blog/ai-freight-forwarding-quote-automation)). That is surely high for simple lanes. My estimate for simple sea freight: 15 to 45 minutes each, 10 to 50 a day.

**e. Who and cost.** Pricing or sales executives, often ₹25,000 to ₹45,000 a month (my estimate, no source).

**f. Tools and gap.** Crowded: FreightMynd, Wisor, Cargofive, Info-X, Shipthis all sell AI quoting ([FreightMynd](https://freightmynd.com/blog/best-ai-freight-quoting-software-2026/), [Wisor](https://wisor.ai/solution/freight-email-automation/), [Cargofive](https://cargofive.com/ai-powered-freight-quoting/), [Info-X](https://infox.com/rate-quote-automation), [Shipthis](https://www.shipthis.co/solutions/freight-quote-management-software)). Gap: small forwarders whose rates live in messy Excel.

**g. Price.** ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 setup, ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 a month.

**h. Sources.** Inline above.

**Verdict.** Only do this if a forwarder you know asks for it. Rates change weekly, so the data is unstable. That is a support burden two part time people will feel.

### Price check: your guess, confirmed with a volume rule

What UK buyers see from other sellers in 2026:
* Simple workflows start around £500. Mid range builds run £3,000 to £10,000. Monthly support usually runs £200 to £800, which is about 265 to 1,060 USD. (These figures came from one search across these UK pricing guides: [AI Workforce](https://www.aiworkforce.co.uk/blogs/ai-automation-pricing-uk-small-businesses-2026), [Axivon](https://www.axivon-ai.com/blog/ai-automation-agency-cost-uk), [Optimised](https://getoptimised.co.uk/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-automate-a-small-business).)

My own math gives far less for pure keying (about £2.20 for an 8 minute order), so their figure must include errors and overheads. Use your own math in the pitch, not theirs.

**Selling line that now adds up:** "It costs less than one day a week of your order clerk." One day a week is about 32 hours a month, or about £540 (about 710 USD) at the loaded rate above. The 400 and 700 USD tiers are below that.

### Europe workflow 1 (top pick): Customer purchase order email to draft sales order, for Sage 200, Exact Online and Unleashed users

**a. Who has it.** Wholesalers and distributors (food service, building supplies, electrical, industrial parts, packaging, beauty and pet products), and manufacturers who sell to trade customers. **Medium** is the sweet spot: a customer service or sales order team of 2 to 8 people, owned by the operations or customer service manager. **Small** fits only above about 500 emailed orders a month.

**b. Steps today.**
1. Customer emails a PO as PDF, Excel, or plain text in the email body ([Y Meadows](https://ymeadows.com/en/insights/blog/ai-order-entry-for-distributors/)).
2. Staff open it, find the customer in the ERP, and match each line to a product code, often from the customer's own part numbers.
3. They check prices against the price list, check stock, and key the sales order.
4. They send an order confirmation.

**c. Software.** In: Outlook or Gmail. Out: Sage 200 (SOP module), Exact Online, or Unleashed. Middle: our approval screen, or the ERP's own draft or quote status where it has one. That keeps it to 2 or 3 tools.

**d. How often, how long.** A medium distributor gets hundreds to thousands of emailed orders a month. My estimate: 5 to 15 minutes of keying per order (8 used in the math above), plus fixing errors later. Vendors put each order error at 250 USD or more ([Y Meadows](https://use.ymeadows.com/cost_of_errors?_bhlid=demo-link)). **Count it:** pull 4 weeks of the orders inbox and 4 weeks of sales orders created by user. Time 20 orders with a stopwatch.

**e. Who does it and what it costs.** Sales order processors, customer service coordinators, sales administrators, at about £21,000 to £27,000 a year in the UK, or about £2,380 a month loaded on a £25,000 salary. In the Netherlands, about €2,545 gross a month. At 1,000 orders a month and 8 minutes each, that is about 133 hours, most of one person, about 2,920 USD a month. Sources in the cost table above.

**f. Existing tools and the gap.**
* **This market is crowded.** Canals, Y Meadows, Graip, WizCommerce, Endeavor and others sell AI order entry ([WizCommerce list](https://wizcommerce.com/blog/top-ai-order-entry-automation-software/), [Oro](https://oroinc.com/b2b-ecommerce/blog/sales-order-automation/), [Canals](https://www.canals.ai/products/distributor-order-entry), [Endeavor](https://www.endeavor.ai/sales-order-automation)). Graip is Latvian and serves European firms on SAP, Oracle and Dynamics ([RIGA COMM](https://rigacomm.com/en/meet-graip-ai-born-in-latvia-built-for-the-world/), [Graip](https://graip.ai/wholesale-distribution)). Business Central, Cin7 and Odoo users have it built in or cheap.
* **The gap, honestly stated:**
  1. The funded vendors aim at SAP, Oracle, Dynamics and larger budgets with custom quotes. For a 60 person UK distributor on Sage 200 or Unleashed, the choices are a UK dev shop like Zestcode, a niche add on like Logistify, or a clerk. You compete on price and speed of setup, not on a unique product.
  2. The hard part is not reading the PDF. It is the customer part number to SKU mapping, units (case vs each), price list rules, and the ERP write. That needs hands on setup per client. That is a service, which is what you sell.
* **If a client already pays for one of these tools and is happy, walk away.**

**g. Realistic price.** From the one price table: 2,000 USD build for your first 2 EU clients, then 3,000 to 4,000 USD. Monthly 400 USD (500 to 1,000 orders), 700 USD (1,000 to 2,500), 1,000 to 1,500 USD above that.

**h. Sources.** Inline above.

**Edge cases this build must handle on day 1:** multi page PDFs; Excel orders; orders in the email body; two POs in one email; customer resends the same PO; new delivery address (flag, never create); price differs from price list (flag); unknown product (flag); German, Dutch or French text (the AI reads it; the approval screen stays in English). Amendments and cancellations are flagged, not applied, in version 1.

**Smallest version that works.**
1. Week 1 to 2: shadow mode. Parse orders from a forwarded inbox into the approval screen. Staff keep working. Measure accuracy per field.
2. Week 3 to 4: approved drafts written to the ERP for the top 10 customers by volume.
3. Week 5 to 6: all customers, exceptions list, duplicate check, daily summary email to the process owner.
4. Later, paid as a change: amendments, order confirmations back to the customer.

### Europe workflow 2 (second product, and the lead pitch for Business Central users): Supplier order confirmation checked against our purchase order

**a. Who.** Manufacturers and distributors that raise many purchase orders: engineering, food production, furniture, electrical wholesale. **Medium** mostly; the purchasing manager owns it. **Small** only if they raise 20+ POs a week.

**b. Steps today.** Buyer sends a PO from the ERP. Supplier replies with an order confirmation PDF. It lands in one person's inbox and often gets checked late or not at all ([Cflow](https://www.cflowapps.com/purchase-order-acknowledgement/)). A purchasing administrator compares price, quantity and delivery date line by line, chases missing confirmations, and updates the ERP.

**c. Software.** Outlook plus Business Central, Sage 200, Exact or Odoo. Output: confirmed dates written back to the PO after approval, and a mismatch list for the buyer. Business Central has a Payables Agent for supplier invoices, but I found no built in agent for supplier order confirmations.

**d. How often, how long.** Tens to hundreds of confirmations a week. My estimate: 5 to 10 minutes each when done properly. **Count it:** POs raised per month, and how many confirmations sit unread in the inbox.

**e. Who and cost.** Purchasing administrators and assistants at about £22,000 to £30,000 a year in the UK ([Reed](https://www.reed.co.uk/average-salary/average-purchasing-administrator-salary), [Indeed UK](https://uk.indeed.com/career/purchasing-assistant/salaries)). Job postings list "chasing order acknowledgements" and "reviewing supplier order acknowledgements and resolving discrepancies" as core duties, so firms pay salary for exactly this ([Supply Chain Online posting](https://www.supplychainonline.co.uk/job/792299/purchasing-administrator/?TrackID=590), [South West Recruitment](https://www.swr.uk.net/jobs/purchasing-administrator-8489/)). Example: 300 confirmations a month at 7 minutes is 35 hours, about £580 (about 770 USD) a month at the loaded rate. The bigger cost is a supplier price rise nobody noticed, or a late delivery nobody saw coming.

**f. Existing tools and gap.** SER, Doxis and free-com automate this, but they are document management suites for bigger firms ([SER](https://www.sergroup.com/en/solutions/order-confirmation-automation.html), [Doxis](https://www.doxis.com/en/solutions/order-confirmation-automation), [free-com](https://free-com.eu/en/products/order-confirmations/)). I found no cheap SMB tool that does this for Business Central, Sage 200 or Exact users.

**g. Price.** From the one price table: 2,000 USD build as a second workflow (3,000 USD if it is the client's first), 400 to 700 USD a month.

**h. Sources.** Inline above.

**Edge case that kills the deal:** if nobody checks confirmations today, the process is "skipped", which you said you do not want. **Only sell where a named person already checks them and complains about it.** Ask: "Who checked the last supplier price increase, and how did you find it?"

## Part 4: Final picks and plan

### Pick 1: Order to draft sales order (India first, then Europe on Sage 200, Exact Online, Unleashed)
**Why:** It passed every filter. Data already arrives digitally. One owner. Two or three tools. The approval screen keeps a human in control. The business keeps running if we fail. It is countable per order, and the savings table shows it pays for itself from about 500 orders a month. The same engine sells in both regions, so Indian proof helps European sales. Developer A takes the ERP writes (Tally agent, Sage 200, Exact), matching logic and AWS; Developer B takes WhatsApp coexistence and email intake, n8n plumbing and the approval screen.
**The honest catch:** in Europe you are a cheaper, hands on alternative to UK service shops and add ons, not the only option. Business Central, Cin7 and Odoo users are off the list for this workflow.

### Pick 2: Supplier confirmation check (Europe only)
**Why:** A real gap between enterprise suites and nothing. It reuses Pick 1's parsing and ERP connectors. It is the product you lead with for Business Central users, and the second sale to Pick 1 clients who are medium manufacturers.

### Not picked
Freight quoting (crowded, unstable data), all accounts payable and remittance ideas (cheap tools exist), timesheets (deadline plus personal data), Tally purchase entry (TaxOne), order entry on Business Central, Cin7 or Odoo (built in or cheap).

### Your 90 day plan

1. **Days 1 to 15.** Find 2 Indian distributors on Tally with at least 80 orders a day, most of them in 1:1 WhatsApp chats or email. Ludhiana is full of them. Turn on coexistence, sync history, run the go or no go count, then run shadow mode free. Record every number.
2. **Days 15 to 45.** Go live with approve and post at the first one. Get a written quote and screen recording.
3. **Days 30 to 60.** In parallel, contact UK and Dutch distributors on **Sage 200, Exact Online or Unleashed**. Ask two questions: "How many emailed orders a month, and how many people key them?" and "Have you tried any tool or add on for this?" Contact Business Central manufacturers with the supplier confirmation pitch instead. Aim for 5 real calls.
4. **Days 60 to 90.** Close one European client at 2,000 USD build plus 400 or 700 USD a month, by their volume.

### Stop signs (be honest with yourselves)
* No European sales call in 60 days: the problem is reach, not the product. Fix sales before building more.
* Shadow mode accuracy below 85% of lines correct after 2 weeks of mapping fixes: the client's data is too messy. Do not go live.
* Indian client whose orders are mostly voice notes or group chats: v1 cannot help yet. Say no or wait.
* A European prospect whose ERP already has an order agent or who uses an add on they like: switch to Pick 2 or walk away.
* Any client whose ERP has no API: quote the connector cost or say no.
* More than 4 hours a week of support per client: raise the monthly fee or narrow the scope.

### Staying under 50 USD a month
One small cloud server running n8n community edition and your Java or TypeScript service, a domain, and pay per use AI calls during pilots. Xero's free API tier covers your first 5 Xero clients. Each client's hosting, AI usage, WhatsApp provider fees and message charges go into their monthly fee from day 1, so growth never breaks the budget. (Server and AI costs are my estimates; check current prices when you sign up.)
