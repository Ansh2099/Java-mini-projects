# Workflow Automations SMBs Will Pay For: India and Europe (30 September 2026)

## The short answer

Build one engine and sell it as a few narrow workflows: **read a business document that arrives by email or WhatsApp, match every line to the client's own system, flag what does not match, and let a human approve.**

* **Europe lead (paid): supplier order confirmations checked against your purchase orders.** Medium manufacturers and distributors on Business Central, weclapp, or Exact Online. A buyer compares every confirmation PDF with the PO by hand today. The big ERP AI agents shipped in 2026 do not cover this step.
* **Europe second (paid, narrow): customer PO emails into draft sales orders.** Only for firms on ERPs that do not already ship an AI order agent or a cheap plugin, and only at about 35+ orders a day.
* **India (proof of work):** retailer orders on WhatsApp typed into Tally, Busy, or Marg, and buyer POs typed into Tally or Zoho Books by manufacturers and exporters.

**The hard truth, stated plainly.** Order entry on the sales side is being built into ERPs right now. Microsoft's Business Central Sales Order Agent is generally available and costs cents per order. Odoo has several AI "PO to quotation" apps. If a client's ERP already does it, you cannot sell it, and you must check this before every sales call. Your edge is not the AI. It is the setup work that software skips (each customer's item codes, odd formats, edge cases) and the workflows that ERP vendors have not reached yet. Keep contracts short, because that list will shrink.

Why not invoices: across Europe, supplier invoices are being forced into structured e-invoice formats by law (Germany, Belgium, Poland, France), and invoice capture is already cheap (Hubdoc comes with Xero; Dext starts around £24 a month). No law covers purchase orders, order confirmations, or WhatsApp orders.

## Assumptions I made

1. "Would pay for" means paying you as a service (build fee plus monthly upkeep), not buying a SaaS license.
2. "Europe" means the EU plus the UK, English speaking buyers first (UK, Ireland, Netherlands, Nordics). Germany is the biggest prize but needs German.
3. "A few weeks of part time work" means about 60 to 120 hours for the first build.
4. "Evidence of spend" can be salaries, job ads, or paid tools for the same task.
5. Your 50 USD a month budget covers your own tools. Once a client pays, their running costs go into their fee.
6. Currency: £1 is about $1.32 (GBP/USD closed near 1.3224 on 30 September 2026, [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-kingdom/currency)).

## The filter every idea went through

An idea passed only if it met all of these:

1. One process, clear start and end.
2. Two to three tools at most.
3. One owner who feels the pain.
4. A human approves exceptions.
5. Buildable in a few weeks part time.
6. Countable (how often, how long).
7. Already part digital: the data exists in email, PDF, Excel, WhatsApp, or software, and a person retypes it.
8. **Not already solved by a built in feature or a cheap tool, checked for the client's exact ERP and edition.** This includes the ERP vendor's own AI agents and its app store.

## How every price in this document is set

One formula, used everywhere, so you can redo it with each client's numbers:

1. **Monthly saving** = hours the task takes each month × the loaded cost of one hour × 60 percent. I count only 60 percent of the time as removed, because a human still reviews exceptions. (Assumption. Vendors claim 80 percent or more; I stay below that.)
2. **Monthly fee** is at most one third of the monthly saving. The client keeps two thirds.
3. **Build fee** is at most about six months of saving. (Assumption.)
4. Error costs (credit notes, returns, wrong prices) count only if the client can show them from their own records.

**UK loaded cost of one employee.** Employer National Insurance is 15 percent of pay above £5,000 in 2026/27 ([Employer Calculator](https://employercalculator.co.uk/guides/employer-ni-rates-2026-27), [Protax](https://www.protax.org.uk/articles/employer-national-insurance-2026-rates/)). Add 3 percent employer pension on pay above about £6,240 (Assumption on the pension band). A full time week is 37.5 hours (Assumption).

# Part 1: India

## India pick 1: WhatsApp orders from retailers into Tally, Busy, or Marg

**Verdict: good for proof of work. Sell it only where volume is high or mistakes are expensive. It will not fund you.**

**a. Who has it.** Distributors and wholesalers of FMCG goods, pharma, hardware and sanitary goods, electricals, and auto spare parts. Fits **small** (owner run, 10 to 40 staff) and **medium** distributors (50+ staff, a billing or sales ops head owns it). WhatsApp is already the default order channel for Indian distributors ([Fast2SMS](https://www.fast2sms.com/help/tally-whatsapp-for-distributors/), [Sunray Datalinks](https://sunraydatalinks.com/blog/whatsapp-order-management-system-for-distributors-wholesalers/)).

**b. Steps today.**
1. A retailer or field salesman sends an order on WhatsApp: typed Hinglish, a photo of a handwritten list, or a voice note, often at odd hours ([LineNow](https://www.linenow.co/blog/guides/whatsapp-supplier-ordering-smb)).
2. A billing clerk works out which item "Surf 1kg bada" means in the item master and checks stock and that retailer's rate or scheme.
3. The clerk types a sales order or invoice into Tally, Busy, or Marg.
4. Someone confirms back on WhatsApp and passes it to dispatch.

Data ends up in the accounting software. The WhatsApp chat is the only record of what was asked for.

**c. Software on each side, and how you actually connect.**
* **In:** WhatsApp Business app. Meta's "coexistence" lets the same number run on the app and the Cloud API at once, and India is supported, so the client keeps their number and chat history ([MSG91](https://msg91.com/guide/unlock-whatsapp-coexistence-use-whatsapp-business-app-and-cloud-api-together), [Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/embedded-signup/onboarding-business-app-users/)).
* **Out, Marg:** has an API gateway covering orders, bills, stock and outstandings ([Marg API](https://margcompusoft.com/Package/Packages.aspx)). Server to server. No agent needed.
* **Out, Busy:** says it has no default third party integration; you need a channel partner or add on ([Busy FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/third-party-services/integration/1/), [BusyNotify](https://busynotify.in/solutions/custom-apis)). Skip Busy until you have a working route.
* **Out, TallyPrime:** accepts XML over HTTP on port 9000, but only on the machine where Tally runs, and it has no webhooks ([Tally XML integration](https://help.tallysolutions.com/xml-integration/), [AI Accountant guide](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/tally-api-integration-guide)). **Your server cannot call a shop PC's localhost:9000 through its router, and you must never open that port to the internet.** So you build a bridge:
  1. **A small Windows agent** (a Java jar or Node app) installed on the Tally PC, or on the client's "Tally on Cloud" server if they rent one. It makes **outbound HTTPS calls only**: every 30 to 60 seconds it asks your server "any approved orders?", posts each one as XML to http://localhost:9000, and sends Tally's reply (created, or the error text) back. Nothing inbound is opened, so the client's router needs no changes.
  2. **The same agent pulls master data up:** item master, ledger names, stock, and rates, every night and on demand, so matching uses fresh data.
  3. **Tally settings the client must have:** TallyPrime running with the right company open, and the XML port on under F1 > Settings > Connectivity > Client/Server configuration, "TallyPrime acts as" set to Server or Both, port 9000 ([Tallystack guide](https://www.tallystack.in/blog/tally-on-cloud/how-to-connect-tally-prime-to-server/), [Vyapar TaxOne port help](https://taxone.vyapar.com/help/articles/how-to-check-tally-port-or-change-port-in-tally-prime)). Put this in your onboarding checklist and test it on day one.
  4. **Orders go to Tally only after a human approves them** in your review screen, so Tally never holds a half checked voucher.
  5. **If the PC is off or Tally is closed,** approved orders wait in your queue. The agent sends a heartbeat every minute; if none arrives for 30 minutes during business hours, the owner gets a WhatsApp alert. Nothing is lost, and staff can fall back to typing by hand.
  6. This pattern is proven: others already ship Tally agents that talk to localhost:9000 with no inbound ports ([TallyConnect.online](https://www.tallyconnect.online/), [tally-desk-app on GitHub](https://github.com/aggarwalabhishekco/tally-desk-app)). Budget about 20 to 30 hours to build and test yours once. (Assumption.)
* **Best first targets:** Marg users (no agent needed), then Tally users once your agent works.

**d. How often and how long.** (Assumption) A mid size distributor gets 50 to 300 order messages a day, each taking 3 to 6 minutes to decode and type, more for photos. Vendors claim WhatsApp order automation cuts processing time by 60 to 80 percent ([Fast2SMS](https://www.fast2sms.com/help/tally-whatsapp-for-distributors/)).

**e. Who does it and what it costs.** Billing clerks or data entry staff. In Ludhiana, data entry operators with Tally skills are offered ₹15,000 to ₹17,000 a month, and assistant accountants ₹18,000 to ₹25,000 ([Indeed Ludhiana, Tally data entry](https://in.indeed.com/q-accountant-tally,-data-entry-l-ludhiana,-punjab-jobs.html), [Indeed Ludhiana, assistant accountant](https://in.indeed.com/q-assistant-accountant-l-ludhiana,-punjab-jobs.html)).

**Brutal honesty:** at 50 orders a day this is less than half a clerk. The owner will hire a ₹15,000 clerk and he will be right. Automation wins only when volume is high (100+ a day) or peaks before the dispatch cutoff, when wrong items or rates cost real money (pharma batches, scheme mistakes, returns), or when the business depends on one clerk who knows all the item nicknames.

**f. Existing tools and the gap.**
* Order apps like Rupyz, Nexxio Dealer Connect, and HublerX give retailers a catalog and sync to Tally ([Rupyz](https://rupyz.com/blog/order-management-system-with-whatsapp-tally-integration/), [Nexxio](https://nexxio.in/dealer-connect.html), [HublerX](https://www.hublerx.ai/resources/blog/top-whatsapp-order-management-software-manufacturers-india-2026)). **Gap:** they need the retailer to change how they order. Many kirana owners will keep sending a photo of a list.
* Outbound WhatsApp from Tally is solved: TallyPrime with WhatsApp costs ₹1,300 plus GST, and the Fast2SMS plugin is free plus ₹0.25 a message ([TallyHelp price list](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tallyprime-with-whatsapp-price-list/), [Fast2SMS cost](https://www.fast2sms.com/help/tally-whatsapp-cost-calculator/)). **Do not sell outbound messaging.**
* Your gap: **read the messy inbound order as it arrives today**, match every line to the item master and that retailer's rate, and hold anything unclear for a human.

**g. Realistic price (from the formula: 4 minutes an order, ₹16,000 clerk).**

| Orders a day | Monthly saving | Most you can charge a month |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | about ₹4,000 | about ₹1,300 (not worth selling) |
| 100 | about ₹8,000 | about ₹2,700 |
| 150 | about ₹12,000 | about ₹4,000 |
| 300 | about ₹24,000 | about ₹8,000 |

Setup: ₹25,000 to ₹50,000, which is under six months of saving from 150 orders a day. Anchors: Tally customisation jobs run ₹2,000 to ₹50,000+ ([IndiaMART](https://dir.indiamart.com/impcat/tally-customization-services.html)); a TallyPrime Silver license is ₹22,500 one time plus ₹4,500 a year ([Markit Solutions](https://www.markitsolutions.in/blogs/tally-renewal-charges-2026-guide)). Charge more only if the client shows real error costs. For your first 2 or 3 clients, charge at cost in exchange for a written case study with real numbers.

Running cost watch: from **1 October 2026**, Meta starts charging for service and utility messages in the open 24 hour window in India, about ₹0.115 each after a free allowance ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [WatEase](https://watease.com/blog/whatsapp-business-platform-pricing-india)). Sources disagree on the free allowance; check Meta's page ([WhatsApp pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/)). Put it in the monthly fee.

**h. Sources.** Inline above and in the list at the end.

## India pick 2: Buyer purchase orders into sales orders for B2B manufacturers and exporters

**Verdict: the best Indian case study for European buyers, because it is the same workflow as Europe pick 2 and the same engine as Europe pick 1. Small money on its own.**

**a. Who has it.** Auto component, fastener, hand tool, bicycle part, and hosiery makers that sell to OEMs, distributors, or foreign buyers. Ludhiana has over 1.5 lakh registered MSMEs across these trades ([SME Communities](https://smecommunities.com/ludhiana-punjabs-industrial-powerhouse-driving-indias-next-sme-revolution/)); the bicycle industry alone is about ₹3,500 crore of exports ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/ludhiana/cycle-industry-seeks-revival-amid-export-challenges)). Fits **both**: small (owner and one clerk) and medium (sales coordination or export team).

**b. Steps today.**
1. A buyer emails a PO as PDF or Excel. Foreign buyers use their own item codes.
2. A sales coordinator or export documentation executive matches buyer codes to internal items, checks price against the agreed rate list, and checks the delivery date.
3. They type a sales order in Tally or Zoho Books and add a row to the order tracking sheet.
4. For exports, the same data is retyped into a proforma invoice and later the packing list.

**c. Software.** In: Gmail or Outlook with PDF or Excel attachments. Out: **Zoho Books** has a cloud sales order API, no agent needed ([Zoho Books API](https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/sales-order/)); **TallyPrime** needs the Windows agent from India pick 1. Plus an order register sheet. That is 3 tools. **For your very first case study, prefer a Zoho Books client or one on Tally on Cloud,** so you are not debugging an office PC while you prove the engine.

**d. How often and how long.** (Assumption) 5 to 40 POs a week for a small maker, more for a medium one; 15 to 45 minutes each when there are many lines. Measure at the client.

**e. Who does it and what it costs.** A Ludhiana posting offers ₹30,000 to ₹35,000 a month for an export documentation executive with 3 to 5 years' experience ([ABP Empower](https://www.abpempower.in/job-openings-for-export-import-documentation-executive-ludhiana-1299973.htm), [Indeed Ludhiana export jobs](https://in.indeed.com/Export-Import-jobs-in-Ludhiana,-Punjab)). A wrong price or date on a foreign order can cost more than a month's salary, so measured error costs matter here.

**f. Existing tools and the gap.** Export document software (ExpoMaster, EximDoc, Export EMS, Eximly) already generates proformas, invoices, and packing lists ([ExpoMaster](https://expomaster.in/), [EximDoc](https://eximdoc.in/), [Export EMS](https://www.visualexportems.com/), [Eximly](https://www.eximly.in/blog/best-export-import-software-india-comparison)). **Do not sell document generation.** The gap is the step before it: reading the buyer's PO, mapping codes, and checking price and date. None of those tools claim to do that in the results I saw.

**g. Realistic price (formula: 30 minutes a PO, ₹32,500 a month, about ₹156 an hour).**

| POs a week | Monthly saving | Most you can charge a month |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | about ₹4,000 | about ₹1,350 |
| 40 | about ₹8,100 | about ₹2,700 |
| 80 | about ₹16,200 | about ₹5,400 |

Setup ₹25,000 to ₹50,000. Honest read: on time saved alone this is ₹1,500 to ₹5,000 a month. It is worth doing for the case study, not the income. Charge more only against error costs the client can show.

# Part 2: Europe (including the UK)

## Before any European sales call: the qualification check

This is where most bad deals die, so do it before the call, not after:

1. **ERP and edition.** Not just "Exact", but which Exact plan. Not just "Business Central", but Online or on premises.
2. **Does the ERP vendor already ship this?** Search the vendor's own AI agent list and release notes, and its app store (AppSource, Odoo Apps, Exact App Center) for "AI order", "OCR sales order", "order confirmation". If a built in agent or a cheap app covers it, say no and say why. That honesty is a selling point.
3. **Volume.** Count the last 30 days. Use the price tables below. If the numbers do not reach the $400 a month line, say no or point them to a product.
4. **Formats.** What share arrives as PDF, Excel, email body text, or scans.
5. **Owner.** One named person who does it today.
6. **Data location.** They must accept EU hosting with a DPA (see break case 4).

## Europe pick 1 (lead): Supplier order confirmations checked against purchase orders

**Verdict: your lead European product. Medium firms. The 2026 ERP agents stop at the sales side and at invoices; this step in between is still manual below enterprise level.**

**a. Who has it.** Manufacturers and distributors with a purchasing team buying from many suppliers. Mainly **medium** (a purchasing lead owns it). Small firms with one buyer have it too, but usually below the volume that pays for a service.

**b. Steps today.** The ERP sends a PO to the supplier. The supplier replies with an order confirmation PDF (Auftragsbestätigung in German). A buyer opens it and compares every line: item, quantity, price, delivery date. They update the promised date in the ERP and chase the supplier if anything differs. Many POs never get a confirmation, so buyers also chase silence ([Pexon](https://pexon-consulting.de/blog/auftragsbestaetigung-automatisieren/), [Leverage](https://tryleverage.ai/blog/pf/po-acknowledgement-automation-hybrid-edi-1)).

**c. Software on each side, API status, and what is already built in.**
* In: Outlook or Gmail.
* Out: the ERP's purchase order lines. Optional third tool: a Teams or email alert for exceptions.

| ERP | Can you update PO lines by API? | Already built in or cheap? | Sell it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Central | Yes, purchaseOrderLine update in API v2.0 ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/api-reference/v2.0/api/dynamics_purchaseorderline_update)) | **No agent for this.** The Sales Order Agent "can't create or work with" purchase documents ([Microsoft Learn FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/faqs-sales-order-taker-agent), search result text). The Payables Agent matches supplier **invoices** to orders, not confirmations ([Microsoft Learn release plan](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/release-plan/2025wave2/smb/dynamics365-business-central/match-purchase-invoices-orders-payables-agent)). Abakion's Purchase Order Management app makes suppliers type replies into a web portal, which asks the supplier to change how they work ([Abakion](https://abakion.com/dynamics-365/apps/purchase-order-management/)). | **Yes, best target** |
| weclapp | Yes, Purchase Order API ([APIs.io](https://apis.io/providers/weclapp/)) | None found in my searches (Assumption: not searched in depth) | Yes, needs German |
| Exact Online Wholesale Distribution or Manufacturing | (Assumption) purchase order endpoints in the same editions that have sales orders; confirm on the client's plan | None found | Yes, check the edition |
| Odoo | Yes, RPC | AI purchase order digitisation apps exist ([Odoo Apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/16.0/dx_purchase_digitization)). Check whether one handles confirmations against existing POs before you pitch. | Only after checking |
| SAP ECC or S/4 | | Enterprise tools exist (OCC.AI, henrichsen4s) | No, too big for you |

**Risk (Assumption):** Microsoft could add a purchase side agent to Business Central. Read the BC release plan every six months and keep contracts at 3 to 6 months.

**d. How often and how long.** A German case: buyers spent about 15 minutes per confirmation, and one firm (esw) spent 25 to 30 hours a week on it, cut to under 5 hours after automation ([Mittelstand Cafe](https://www.mittelstandcafe.de/perzeptron-automatisiert-ab-kontrolle-bei-esw-pr-faufwand-sinkt-mit-occ-ai-um-ber-80-prozent-2252271.html), [SCM](https://scm-supply-chain-management.de/presse/prufaufwand-sinkt-mit-occ-ai-um-uber-80-prozent/)). At 15 minutes each, 25 to 30 hours is about 100 to 120 confirmations a week. A US vendor says supplier chasing takes 20 to 30 percent of buyer time at mid market manufacturers ([Leverage](https://tryleverage.ai/blog/pf/reduce-supplier-expediting-time)). Both are vendor sources.

**e. Who does it and what it costs.** Buyers and purchasing assistants. UK buyer average £36,748 a year (Indeed, updated 21 September 2026), range about £25,700 to £52,500; purchasing assistant £25,869 ([Indeed buyer](https://uk.indeed.com/career/buyer/salaries), [Indeed purchasing assistant](https://uk.indeed.com/career/purchasing-assistant/salaries), [PayScale buyer](https://www.payscale.com/research/UK/Job=Buyer/Salary)). Loaded: about £42,400 and £29,600.

Worked example like esw: 100 confirmations a week × 15 minutes = 25 hours a week, two thirds of a full time person. Done by a buyer, that is about £28,300 a year; 60 percent removed saves about £17,000 a year, or £1,410 a month. Done by an assistant, about £990 a month saved. This counts only checking time. Chasing missing confirmations is extra value that I do not count.

**f. Existing tools and the gap.** OCC.AI (Perzeptron), Doxis, DocBits, TCG DPS Order Response, and SAP focused add ons ([Doxis](https://www.doxis.com/en/solutions/order-confirmation-automation), [DocBits](https://docbits.com/en/order-confirmation-automation/), [henrichsen4s](https://www.henrichsen4s.de/sap-loesungen/einkauf/auftragsbestaetigung)). Leverage serves US mid market. **None of them publish prices.** DocBits prices by document volume and modules, and Leverage's supply chain product is quote only (search result text, [DocBits](https://docbits.com/en/order-confirmation-automation/), [Leverage quote page](https://pages.tryleverage.ai/price-quote)). Quote only pricing usually means a sales team and a bigger ticket (Assumption). **Gap:** medium firms on Business Central, weclapp, or Exact Wholesale that are too small for an enterprise document platform and want someone to set it up.

**g. Realistic price (formula, 15 minutes each).**

| Confirmations a week | Most you can charge a month (assistant does it) | Most you can charge a month (buyer does it) |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | about £165 ($220) | about £235 ($310) |
| 100 | about £330 ($435) | about £470 ($620) |
| 200 | about £660 ($870) | about £940 ($1,245) |

So: **$400 to $650 a month at about 100 a week, $850 to $1,250 at about 200 a week**, build **$2,500 to $4,000**. Below about 65 a week (buyer) or 95 a week (assistant), the saving cannot carry a $400 fee.

## Europe pick 2 (narrow): Customer PO emails into draft sales orders

**Verdict: real pain and real spend, but ERP vendors and cheap apps now cover the biggest ERPs. Sell it only on uncovered ERPs, at about 35+ orders a day.**

**a. Who has it.** Wholesalers and distributors (industrial, electrical, packaging, food service, building supplies) and small manufacturers selling B2B. Fits **medium** and the **top end of small** (35+ orders a day). Below that, the saving cannot pay a service fee.

**b. Steps today.** Orders arrive as email attachments, spreadsheets, and PDFs in each customer's own format. Someone identifies the customer, looks up item codes, checks price and stock, keys it into the ERP line by line, and sends a confirmation ([ChannelFlex](https://channelflex.com/blog/sales-order-automation-for-distributors-and-manufacturers/), [Y Meadows](https://ymeadows.com/en/insights/blog/ai-order-entry-for-distributors/), [Hyperfox](https://www.hyperfox.com/insights/the-real-cost-of-manual-order-entry)).

**c. Software on each side, API status, and what is already built in.**
* In: Outlook or Gmail (both have good APIs).
* Out: the ERP. This decides whether you may sell at all.

| ERP | Sales order API? | Already built in or cheap? | Sell PO entry? |
|---|---|---|---|

# Where this plan breaks, and the fix

I attacked the plan with hostile cases. Each one broke something. Here is the patch for each.

**1. The AI reads "10" as "100", or maps to the wrong item.**
What breaks: a wrong order ships and the client blames you.
Fix: the system never posts a final order. It creates a **draft only**. Every line is checked against the item master, that customer's or supplier's price, and a normal quantity range. Any unknown code, price difference, or odd quantity puts the whole document on **hold** with the reason shown next to the original text. Totals must match the sum of lines or it holds. If it cannot decide, it asks; it never guesses.

**2. The target system is unreachable (Tally PC off, Tally closed, ERP token expired, API limit reached).**
What breaks: orders silently fail to post.
Fix: every document gets an ID and waits in a queue until the ERP confirms it. The Tally agent sends a heartbeat every minute; no heartbeat for 30 minutes in business hours, or any failed post, alerts the owner within 15 minutes. Every evening the client gets a count: "received 42, drafted 42, posted 40, on hold 2". A mismatch is loud by design. If the automation stops, staff type by hand, the same as today. Nothing is lost.

**3. The ERP vendor already does it, or ships it next year.**
What breaks: the sale, or the client cancels once a cheaper built in agent appears. This already happened: Business Central's Sales Order Agent went generally available in 2026.
Fix: the qualification check before every call (ERP, edition, vendor agents, app store). Pick workflows the vendor has not reached (confirmations today). Use 3 to 6 month contracts, recover the build cost in the setup fee, and review the vendor's release plan every six months.

**4. The buyer says "a clerk is cheaper".**
What breaks: the sale, or you build it and they cancel.
Fix: use the price tables. India: at least 100 orders a day, or proven error costs. Europe: at least about 35 POs a day or about 65 to 95 confirmations a week. Never quote above one third of the measured saving.

**5. GDPR stops a European client from sending data to India.**
What breaks: the deal dies in legal review. India has no EU adequacy decision, so transfers need Standard Contractual Clauses and a transfer risk assessment ([EDPB guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/international-data-transfers_en), [DPDPA.com](https://www.dpdpa.com/blogs/india_eu_data_adequacy_dpdpa_gdpr.html)).
Fix: host each European client in an EU cloud region, use an AI provider with EU processing and a data processing agreement, sign a DPA with SCCs, and keep client data off your laptops. Hand over a one page summary before they ask.

**6. A customer or supplier changes their layout, or a new format appears.**
What breaks: accuracy drops quietly over weeks.
Fix: track the share of lines the human corrects, per sender, every week. Above a set level (for example 5 percent), alert yourselves and fix it. This monthly accuracy report also justifies the upkeep fee.

Two risks you cannot fully patch, so plan around them:
* **Part time support across time zones.** India is UTC+5:30 all year. Until 25 October 2026, and again from 28 March 2027, India is 4.5 hours ahead of the UK and 3.5 hours ahead of Central Europe. **From 25 October 2026 to 28 March 2027, it is 5.5 hours ahead of the UK and 4.5 hours ahead of Central Europe.** That winter period is when your first paid pilot runs. In winter, the UK day of 09:00 to 17:30 is 14:30 to 23:00 in Ludhiana, and the Central European day of 09:00 to 17:00 is 13:30 to 21:30. Offer a fixed support window of 14:30 to 19:30 IST (UK 09:00 to 14:00 in winter), promise next business day fixes outside it, and rely on the safe fallback in point 2.
* **Weak evidence.** Most time and error numbers here come from vendors selling automation, and several key pages could only be read as search result text. That is why the rule at the top matters: measure the client's own numbers before you promise anything.
