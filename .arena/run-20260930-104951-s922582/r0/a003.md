# Workflows Worth Selling: India and Europe (September 2026)

## The short answer

1. **Build one thing first: "customer order arrives by email or WhatsApp, and it becomes a checked sales order in the client's system."** It is the only workflow I found that is clearly painful, clearly paid for today (a full salary), not fully solved for small firms, and the same job in India and Europe. That last part gives you a real case study.
2. **Your second product should be the mirror image: "supplier order confirmation arrives, and it is checked against our purchase order."** Same code, same buyers, medium sized firms, less competition.
3. **Stay away from invoice entry, bank entry, GST reconciliation, payment reminders and supplier statement matching.** Cheap tools and built in features already do these. In India, Tally bill entry costs about ₹10,000 a year from a ready tool. In Europe, Microsoft Business Central now has built in AI agents for both sales orders and supplier invoices.
4. **Your Europe price guess is close.** A setup fee of 1,500 to 4,000 USD is right for small firms. Medium firms can pay 4,000 to 8,000 USD. Monthly upkeep will mostly land at 400 to 900 USD, not 1,500, because ready software sells similar help for 400 to 800 USD a month.
5. **The hardest part is not the code. It is getting the first European client to trust a two person team in Ludhiana.** An Indian case study with real numbers, plus the Finland client, is your way in.

## How to read this

* **Confidence labels.** Every workflow has a confidence level: High, Medium or Low. It tells you how sure I am that businesses will pay you for it. Where confidence changes the decision, I say so.
* **"Estimate"** means I worked the number out myself. It is not from a source. Check it on your first 5 sales calls.
* **Sources.** All links were found through web search on 30 September 2026. Many vendor pages could be seen only as search results, not opened in full, so I treat vendor numbers as vendor claims. Microsoft documentation was read in full.
* **Money.** I use about ₹88 per USD and about 1.33 USD per GBP. Check the rate on the day you quote.

## Assumptions

1. "Europe" means the UK, the EU, Norway and Switzerland. The UK, Germany speaking countries, the Netherlands and the Nordics are the best targets because they buy software, answer email in English and pay on time.
2. "Build in a few weeks part time" means about 60 to 120 hours for the first client, and about half that for the second client with the same workflow.
3. You will run the core pipeline in your own code (Java or Node), with n8n used only for glue if at all. This keeps hosting cheap and avoids license questions about hosting n8n for clients. Check n8n's Sustainable Use License before you host it for anyone.
4. Your costs stay under 50 USD a month: one small server (about 5 to 20 USD) plus LLM calls (estimate: 1 to 5 US cents per order document). This holds until you have several busy clients.

## Part 1: India

**The honest picture.** Indian owners will pay, but little, and only when the gain is obvious. A fresher Tally operator costs about ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 a month ([PW Skills](https://pwskills.com/blog/tally/tally-operator-salary-work-job-for-freshers)). Your yearly price must be well below one salary, or the owner will just hire one more person. Also, cheap Indian SaaS products already cover the most common jobs. So in India, pick clients where speed and mistakes cost more than a salary, and treat most of this work as proof for Europe.

**One technical fact that shapes every Indian build.** TallyPrime talks to other software through XML over HTTP on port 9000 on the client's own computer. There is no cloud login ([AI Accountant guide](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/tally-api-integration-guide), [TallyHelp JSON integration](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime-integration-using-json-1/), [TallyPrime API Explorer](https://tallysolutions.com/tallyprime-api-explorer/)). So you need a small agent program on the client's Windows PC that pulls approved entries from your server and posts them into Tally. BUSY has no default integration option and sends you to its channel partners ([BUSY FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/third-party-services/integration/1/)), though third parties sell BUSY APIs ([BusyNotify](https://busynotify.in/solutions/custom-apis), [RootFi](https://www.rootfi.dev/integrations/busy-accounting)). Plan for Tally first. Treat BUSY as extra work.

### India workflow 1: B2B order on WhatsApp or email becomes a sales order in Tally or BUSY

**Confidence: Medium.** The pain is real and proven by the number of tools selling a fix. That same crowd is why you must pick your client carefully.

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| a. Who has it | Distributors and wholesalers (FMCG, pharma, hardware, electrical, auto parts). Manufacturers with dealer networks, such as Ludhiana cycle parts, auto parts and hosiery firms. **Size: both.** Small firms (owner decides) are the easy first buyers. Medium manufacturers have a sales coordinator team. |
| b. Steps today | A dealer sends an order as WhatsApp text, a voice note, a photo of a handwritten list, or a PDF by email. A staff member reads it, finds the party and each item in Tally or BUSY, applies the dealer's discount or scheme, checks stock, and types the sales order or invoice. Then they send a confirmation on WhatsApp. **With you:** the AI reads the message, matches items to the item master, builds a draft order, and flags anything unsure (unknown item, odd quantity, price below the dealer's rate, low stock). A staff member approves in one screen. The agent posts it into Tally. |
| c. Software | In: WhatsApp Business app or WhatsApp Business API, Gmail. Out: TallyPrime (local XML), BUSY (via partner API), sometimes Marg or Zoho Books. |
| d. Volume and time | Estimate: 30 to 300 orders a day for a distributor, 2 to 5 minutes each. Handwritten photos and voice notes take longest. |
| e. Who and cost | Billing clerk or Tally operator, often 1 to 3 people. A fresher costs about ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 a month ([PW Skills](https://pwskills.com/blog/tally/tally-operator-salary-work-job-for-freshers)). |
| f. Existing tools and gap | Ready products already do this: [Karvan.ai](https://www.karvan.ai/) reads text, voice notes and handwritten photos and handles Hindi short forms; [Zotok](https://www.zotok.ai/) turns images and voice into orders and lists from about ₹6,000 ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/zotok-ai)); [Fast2SMS](https://www.fast2sms.com/help/tally-whatsapp-for-distributors/), [HublerX](https://www.hublerx.ai/resources/blog/top-whatsapp-order-management-software-manufacturers-india-2026) and [Wa-Busy](https://wabusy.in/) cover parts of it. **The gap is narrow:** these are platforms the client must move onto. Your opening is a firm that will not switch platforms, runs BUSY or a custom item list the products handle badly, or gets orders as emailed PDFs from big buyers (for example, a manufacturer supplying OEMs). Do not compete for the plain "FMCG distributor on WhatsApp plus Tally" client. The products are cheaper than you. |
| g. Price | ₹25,000 to ₹60,000 setup, plus ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 a month. For your first one or two clients, charge little or nothing in return for permission to publish before and after numbers. |
| h. Sources | Linked in the rows above. |

**Why it matters anyway:** this is the same job as Europe workflow 1. A case study that says "a Ludhiana manufacturer's orders now go from inbox to ERP in 40 seconds with a human check, and typing errors fell from X to Y" sells directly to a UK wholesaler.

## Part 2: Europe (including the UK)

**The honest picture.** Europe pays well for the right workflow, but the software there moves fast. Microsoft Business Central, a very common ERP for European SMEs, now has a built in **Sales Order Agent** that reads customer emails and PDF purchase orders and creates quotes and orders ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/sales-order-agent)). It also has a **Payables Agent** for supplier invoices ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/payables-agent)). Both are billed per use in Copilot Credits ([billing page](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/administration/tenant-admin-center-manage-consumption-billing)). So you must know which ERP a prospect runs before you pitch.

### Europe workflow 1: Customer purchase order by email becomes a checked sales order in the ERP

**Confidence: Medium to High** that the pain is real and paid for. **Medium** that SMEs will buy it from you without a case study.

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| a. Who has it | Wholesalers and distributors (food and drink, building, plumbing and electrical supplies, industrial parts, packaging). B2B manufacturers with repeat trade customers. **Size: both.** Sweet spot: 20 to 150 staff with 30 or more emailed orders a day. Small: the owner or office manager buys. Medium: the customer service or sales office manager owns it. |
| b. Steps today | A customer emails a PO as a PDF, an Excel file, or plain text in the email. An order processor opens it, finds the customer account, translates the customer's product codes into their own item codes, checks the price against the price list, checks stock, types every line into the ERP, and emails a confirmation. **With you:** the AI reads the order, matches customer and items (learning each customer's codes), checks price and stock, and builds a draft order. It auto flags unknown items, price differences, new delivery addresses and short stock. The processor approves or fixes in one screen. The order goes into the ERP by API and a confirmation draft is ready to send. |
| c. Software | In: Microsoft 365 Outlook or Gmail. Out: Sage 200, Xero with an inventory app (Unleashed, Cin7, Katana), Odoo, Exact Online, QuickBooks, Business Central. **APIs:** Xero, Unleashed, Cin7, Katana, Odoo, Exact Online and QuickBooks all have public APIs (from my own knowledge; confirm for each client). Sage 200 has an API, but on premise setups vary, so check before quoting. Business Central has a documented sales order API ([create salesOrders](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/api-reference/v2.0/api/dynamics_salesorder_create)). |
| d. Volume and time | A UK vendor claims at least 80% of sales orders in a typical UK company now arrive by email as PDF, XML, EDI or CSV ([Document Genetics](https://www.document-genetics.co.uk/solutions/automating-the-receipt-of-sales-orders)). Estimate: 3 to 15 minutes per order depending on line count; 20 to 200 orders a day. |
| e. Who and cost | A sales order processor. UK average about £26,832 a year ([Indeed UK, May 2026](https://uk.indeed.com/career/order-processor/salaries)); a UK recruiter says £26,000 to £29,000 ([Prime Appointments](https://prime-appointments.co.uk/sales-order-processor)). With employer costs, one processor costs roughly £32,000 to £36,000 a year (estimate). |
| f. Existing tools and gap | **Enterprise:** Conexiom, reported at 10,000 to 100,000+ USD a year with per trading partner fees ([OrderDrafter comparison](https://orderdrafter.com/alternatives/conexiom-alternative/), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/205134/Conexiom/)). **SMB SaaS:** OrderDrafter at 399 USD a month for about 300 orders and 799 USD for about 1,000, but built for US distributors on Epicor Prophet 21 ([OrderDrafter](https://orderdrafter.com/)). **Extraction only:** Parseur from 39 USD a month reads documents but does not match items or apply ERP rules ([Parseur pricing](https://parseur.com/pricing)). **Built in:** Business Central's Sales Order Agent. Its limits, from Microsoft: it reads email only from a Microsoft Exchange shared mailbox, processes PDF and image attachments (not Excel), finds the customer only by matching the sender's email address, and forwarded emails need a manual fix ([FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/faqs-sales-order-taker-agent)). Its item matching improved in 2026 ([release plan](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/release-plan/2026wave1/smb/dynamics365-business-central/run-multiple-sales-order-agents-per-company-smarter-item-matching-relevance-filtering-streamlined-setup)). **Your gap:** UK and EU SMEs on Sage 200, Xero plus inventory apps, Odoo or Exact; firms whose customers send Excel orders; firms with customer specific codes and price rules. **Do not pitch Business Central firms** unless their orders break the agent's limits. |
| g. Price | Setup 2,500 to 5,000 USD for small, 5,000 to 8,000 USD for medium. Upkeep 400 to 900 USD a month. Stay under the OrderDrafter anchor unless you add real custom logic. The math for the buyer: if you remove half the typing for one processor, that is about £16,000 a year saved (about 21,000 USD) against about 7,000 to 19,000 USD in year one cost (estimate). |

## Part 3: Workflows in both regions (your case study bridge)

| India workflow | Europe workflow | Case study value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. WhatsApp or email order into Tally | 1. Emailed PO into ERP | **High** | Same job: read a messy order, match items, check price and stock, human approves, post to the system. Only the input channel and the ERP change. |
| 3. Chasing suppliers for dispatch dates | 2. Supplier confirmation checked against PO | **Medium** | Same logic of matching supplier replies to PO lines. The India version is unproven, so it may never become a case study. |

## Part 4: My top picks for you

### Pick 1: Order to ERP (Europe workflow 1, proven first in India workflow 1)

* **Why:** It has the clearest proof of paid pain (a £27,000 a year role that mostly types), a real gap for SMEs not on Business Central, and a clean human approval step. It fits both of you: Developer A builds the matching and ERP posting in Spring Boot with Spring AI; Developer B builds the email or WhatsApp intake and the approval screen in TypeScript.
* **Main risk:** Microsoft or your client's ERP vendor adds the same feature. Reduce it by choosing clients on Sage 200, Xero plus inventory apps, Odoo or Exact, and by owning the customer specific rules (codes, prices, delivery rules), which built in agents handle poorly.
* **Chance it works:** I would put it at about a 50 to 60% chance of landing a paying European client within 6 months if you get one strong Indian case study and use your Finland contact for introductions. This is a judgement, not a sourced number.

### Pick 2: Supplier confirmation check (Europe workflow 2)

* **Why:** It reuses most of Pick 1's code (my estimate: about two thirds) (reading documents, matching lines, approval screen, ERP API). The competition sells mostly to SAP customers, and medium firms feel the pain in late deliveries, not just typing time.
* **When:** Sell it as the second workflow to your Pick 1 clients, or lead with it for medium manufacturers in Germany, the Netherlands and the Nordics.

### Pick 3: Nothing else yet

Freight quotes and the India PO chasing idea stay on the "only with a warm lead" list. Everything in the rejected tables is off the list. Two focused products beat five half built ones for a part time team.

### What to do in the next 30 days

1. Find 2 Indian firms for Pick 1: one Tally distributor that dislikes WhatsApp order platforms, and one manufacturer receiving emailed PDF POs. Offer the build free or at cost for case study rights.
2. Before building, ask each for one week of real orders. Count them and time 20 by hand. That checks my estimates.
3. In parallel, message 30 UK or EU wholesalers and distributors on Sage 200, Xero plus Unleashed or Cin7, Odoo or Exact. Ask one question: "How many orders a day does your team retype from email?" Skip anyone on Business Central who is happy with the Sales Order Agent.
4. Prepare a simple data protection agreement and use an EU hosted model endpoint for European data. European buyers will ask about GDPR before price.
