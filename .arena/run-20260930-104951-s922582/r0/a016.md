# Workflows Worth Selling: India and Europe (September 2026)

## The short answer

Most of the ideas people suggest first are already taken by cheap tools. After checking, three workflows are still worth your time. One of them is clearly the best fit for two part-time developers.

**Pick 1 (build this first): supplier statement reconciliation.** A supplier sends a monthly statement. Someone checks every line against the ledger, finds missing invoices, missing credit notes and wrong amounts, and emails the supplier. You sell it in the UK and Europe to medium firms with 150 or more active suppliers. You prove it first in India as "party ledger reconciliation" in Tally, which is the same job with the same matching engine.

**Pick 2 (second offer, narrow niche only): emailed customer purchase order to draft sales order.** Real pain and good money, but at least eight funded products now do this. Only sell it where those products do not connect well, and price it so the client earns the fee back in about 3 months.

**Pick 3 (India only, local income): export shipment document cross-check** for Ludhiana exporters. It checks the customs agent's checklist and the draft bill of lading against your invoice and packing list before anyone approves them. It is useful and local. It will not help you sell in Europe.

**Your Europe price guess is half right.** 1,500 to 4,000 USD for the first workflow is fine for medium firms. 400 to 1,500 USD a month for upkeep is too high for one workflow at most small and medium firms. Plan on 250 to 700 USD a month, and 1,500 only for a high-volume order-entry client. Details are in the pricing section.

**The hardest part is not the workflow.** It is getting a European finance manager to trust two unknown developers in Ludhiana. The plan at the end deals with that directly.

## Assumptions

1. "Europe" means the UK plus EU countries where finance teams work in English or use software with English APIs (UK, Ireland, Netherlands, Nordics, Belgium). Germany and France are harder for you because of language and local accounting tools.
2. Exchange rates used: 1 GBP = 1.33 USD, 1 USD = 88 INR. Rounded.
3. "Loaded" staff cost = salary plus about 20% for employer taxes and pension in the UK. 1,950 working hours a year.
4. Where I give minutes per task without a source, it is marked **(estimate, measure in discovery)**. Do not quote those numbers to a buyer until you have timed them at a real client.
5. About sources: every link below came up in my web searches in September 2026. Many of these sites blocked direct page opening from my research setup, so several figures come from the page text shown in search results, not from reading the full page. Open the three or four numbers you plan to put in a sales deck and check them yourself first.

**What survives, and why.** The ideas that die are all "read a document and type it in." That layer is getting cheap fast: every accounting tool is adding AI capture, and e-invoicing is removing the PDF. What stays hard is the step after that: **comparing two records that come from two different companies, sorting out the differences, and writing to the other party.** No single company's software owns both sides, so no built-in feature can fully solve it. That is where the three picks come from.

## Step 2: Europe (including the UK)

### E1. Supplier statement reconciliation (TOP PICK)

**a. Who has it, and size.**
Wholesalers, distributors, manufacturers, construction contractors, multi-site hospitality groups, and any firm that buys on credit from many suppliers. **Medium (50 to 250 staff): strong fit.** Small (5 to 49): only if they have 100+ supplier accounts, for example a builders' merchant or a restaurant group. A 12-person office with 30 suppliers does not need this.

**b. Steps today.**
1. Start: a supplier emails a statement (PDF, sometimes Excel) to the accounts inbox, usually in the first week of the month.
2. A purchase ledger clerk opens the supplier's account in the accounting system.
3. They tick each statement line against the ledger: invoices, credit notes, payments.
4. For lines on the statement but not in the ledger, they search email for the invoice, or ask the supplier for a copy.
5. For amounts that differ, they check the original invoice and the purchase order.
6. They email the supplier about missing credits or wrong amounts.
7. End: the reconciliation is saved (often an Excel sheet), and the account is marked as agreed or "differences open."

Fiscal (a UK vendor in this space) says most teams only manage to reconcile their top 10 to 20 suppliers, and the rest go unchecked. ([Fiscal guide](https://fiscaltec.com/supplier-statement-reconciliation-ultimate-guide/), [Fiscal: what the process is](https://fiscaltec.com/supplier-statement-reconciliation-what-it-is-why-it-matters-for-ap/))

**c. Software on each side.**
* Inbox: Microsoft 365 (Outlook, read with the Microsoft Graph API) or Google Workspace (Gmail API).
* Ledger, any one of:
  * Xero: public API. Since 2 March 2026, Xero charges developers by tier. The free Starter tier covers 5 connected organisations, which is enough for your first 5 clients. The Advanced tier is about 895 USD a month. ([Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/xero-shifts-to-tiered-pricing-model-for-developers), [Apideck on Xero pricing](https://www.apideck.com/blog/xero-api-pricing-and-the-app-partner-program), [Xero developer pricing page](https://developer.xero.com/pricing))
  * Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central: standard REST API v2.0 plus OData v4 pages for ledger entries. Good for medium firms. ([Knit guide to the BC API](https://getknit.dev/blog/microsoft-business-central-api-guide-in-depth/), [BC standard API examples](https://yzhums.com/6117/))
  * QuickBooks Online: public API.
  * Sage 50 (UK desktop): **avoid.** No REST API. Writing data needs the Sage Data Objects SDK and a Sage Developer Programme subscription from about £3,100 plus VAT a year, or a paid bridge such as HyperAccounts. ([Hyperext on Sage 50 API](https://www.hyperext.com/sage-50-integration/does-sage-50-have-an-api-yes-heres-what-you-need-to-know/))
  * Odoo Online: external API only on the Custom plan, not on Standard. Check the plan before you quote. ([Odoo external API docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/developer/reference/external_api.html), [Odoo forum thread](https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/does-the-external-api-really-require-the-custom-plan-on-odoo-online-or-is-there-a-trial-window-306032))
* That is 2 tools (inbox plus ledger), 3 if they keep a shared Excel reconciliation file.

**d. How often and how long.**
Each supplier, once a month. Fiscal puts the average manual statement at about 30 minutes, and a complex one at around 2 hours. ([Fiscal on cost of errors](https://fiscaltec.com/the-cost-of-errors-in-statement-reconciliation-what-ap-teams-must-know/), [Fiscal fact sheet, May 2026](https://fiscaltec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Statement-Reconciliation-Fact-Sheet-May-2026.pdf), [Klearstack](https://klearstack.com/blogs/supplier-invoice-reconciliation-process-best-practices))

**e. Who does it and what it costs.**
A purchase ledger clerk. Average UK salary £26,802 (Indeed data, updated 1 August 2026). Robert Half gives £26,000 to £32,750 outside London and £35,250 to £44,500 in London. Current job ads list "supplier statement reconciliations" as a named duty. ([Robert Half](https://www.roberthalf.com/gb/en/job-details/purchase-ledger-clerk), [Indeed UK salary page](https://uk.indeed.com/career/purchase-ledger-clerk/salaries))
Loaded cost is about £16 an hour.

**Now the brutal part: the time saving alone is small.**

| Case | Statements a month | Manual hours | Labour cost a month |
|---|---|---|---|
| They only do the top 20 today | 20 × 30 min | 10 h | about £160 |
| They should do all 150 | 150 × 30 min | 75 h | about £1,200 |

If you sell on "we save your clerk 10 hours," you can charge maybe £100 a month. You must sell on two other things:
1. **Coverage:** check 100% of statements, not the top 20. That is work they know they should do and cannot staff.
2. **Money found:** Fiscal says its users find missing credits averaging £13,000 and missing invoices averaging £8,000. Treat those as a vendor's own marketing claims, not audited facts, but they show where the value sits. ([Fiscal on cost of errors](https://fiscaltec.com/the-cost-of-errors-in-statement-reconciliation-what-ap-teams-must-know/), [Fiscal fact sheet](https://fiscaltec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Statement-Reconciliation-Fact-Sheet-May-2026.pdf))

**f. Existing tools and the gap.**
* Fiscal (Fiscaltec): full accounts payable risk platform aimed at larger finance teams. ([Fiscal statement reconciliation](https://fiscaltec.com/solutions/statement-reconciliation/))
* Kefron AP: statement module priced by volume, sold inside a full accounts payable suite. ([Kefron module](https://kefron.com/finance-automation/accounts-payable-automation/features/automated-supplier-statement-reconciliation), [Kefron pricing](https://kefron.com/accounts-payable-invoice-automation-solution/ap-pricing/))
* Westgate Moore: "autorecs" software plus an outsourced reconciliation service. The fact that a firm sells this as a service proves people pay to have it done. ([Westgate Moore](https://www.westgatemoore.co.uk/statement-recs/))
* Snyp: supplier statement work for Xero and QuickBooks. ([Snyp](https://snyp.ai/blog/reconciling-supplier-statements))
* Xero itself: no built-in feature. Users are asking for it on Xero's ideas forum. ([Xero product idea](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967139-purchase-orders-bills-inventory/suggestions/47511881-bills-ai-reconciliation-of-supplier-statements), [Caseron on messy Xero supplier accounts](https://caseron.co.uk/xero-messy-supplier-account-reconciliations/))

**The gap:** the serious tools come bundled in a full accounts payable platform that a 60-person wholesaler will not buy just for this. The light tools cover Xero and QuickBooks only. Nobody in this list drafts the chase email to the supplier and waits for approval, then tracks the reply. **Honest warning:** this is not empty ground. You are competing with Snyp and Westgate Moore at the small end. Your edge is "done for you, on your ledger, including the supplier chasing," not a unique idea.

**How you would build it (2 to 4 weeks part time).**
1. Inbox rule: statements go to a shared folder or address. An n8n workflow picks up new attachments.
2. LLM reads the statement into rows: date, document number, type, amount, running balance. Validate: the rows must add up to the closing balance on the statement. If not, send it to a human. This one check catches most bad extractions.
3. Pull the supplier's ledger for the same period from Xero (`GET /Invoices?where=Type=="ACCPAY"`, `/CreditNotes`, `/Payments`) or the Business Central vendor ledger entries.
4. Matching engine in Spring Boot and PostgreSQL (Developer A). Plain code, no AI: normalise invoice numbers (strip spaces, prefixes, leading zeros), match on number plus amount, then amount plus date within 5 days. Tag every leftover as one of: missing invoice, missing credit note, amount difference, payment not on statement, timing difference.
5. Exception screen (Developer B, Node and TypeScript): the clerk sees only the differences. Buttons: Approve, Edit, Ignore.
6. On approve, create a **draft** email to the supplier in Outlook (Graph `createMessage`, not send). The clerk presses send. Log everything.
7. Read only on the ledger. You never post, change or pay anything. This makes security approval much easier.

**g. Realistic price.**
* Build: £1,500 to £2,500 (about 2,000 to 3,300 USD).
* Monthly: £200 to £400 (about 270 to 530 USD), including hosting, model costs, supplier format changes and a monthly summary.
* Pitch line: "We reconcile every supplier statement, every month, and hand your clerk only the differences and draft emails."

**h. Sources:** listed inline above.

### E2. Emailed customer purchase order to draft sales order (SECOND PICK, narrow)

**a. Who has it, and size.**
Wholesalers and distributors (electrical, packaging, industrial supplies, building materials, food service) and manufacturers who sell to other businesses. **Medium: strong fit** (30 to 200 emailed orders a day). Small: fits only if they get 15+ emailed orders a day.

**b. Steps today.**
1. Start: a customer emails a purchase order as PDF, Excel, or plain text to orders@.
2. A sales order processor finds the customer account.
3. They translate the customer's part numbers into their own product codes.
4. They check prices against the price list or contract.
5. They key the order into the ERP line by line.
6. End: they send an order confirmation back to the customer.

**c. Software.** Outlook or Gmail, plus the ERP. Business Central has a `salesOrders` API endpoint ([Knit BC guide](https://getknit.dev/blog/microsoft-business-central-api-guide-in-depth/), [iPaaS BC endpoints](https://support.ipaas.com/en/articles/12314582-microsoft-dynamics-365-business-central-api-endpoints)). Firms on Xero usually run a separate stock system on top, because Xero's accounting API has quotes and purchase orders but no sales order object. Avoid Sage 50 and Odoo Standard for the same API reasons as E1.

**d. How often and how long.** Every order, every day. Manual entry time **(estimate, measure in discovery): 4 to 8 minutes per order** depending on line count.

**e. Who does it and what it costs.** A sales order processor. UK averages: £20,999 (Jobsite), £26,832 (Indeed order processor), London about £24,233 (Glassdoor). ([Jobsite](https://www.jobsite.co.uk/jobs/sales-order-processor), [Indeed](https://uk.indeed.com/career/order-processor/salaries), [Glassdoor London](https://www.glassdoor.co.uk/Salaries/london-united-kingdom-order-processor-salary-SRCH_IL.0,21_IM1035_KO22,37.htm))
Example: 60 orders a day × 5 minutes = 5 hours a day, about 105 hours a month. At about £15 an hour loaded, that is roughly £1,500 a month of labour. **The money case here is much stronger than E1.**

**f. Existing tools and the gap. This is why it is only second.**
* Conexiom: enterprise, quote only, set up per trading partner. ([ERP Research review](https://www.erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/distribution/conexiom), [Tableflow comparison](https://tableflow.com/resources/conexiom-alternative))
* OrderPier: self-serve, reads any layout with no templates, posts to Business Central, QuickBooks, or a webhook. ([OrderPier](https://orderpier.com/))
* Ordermatic, Elentaria, StackCube, ChannelFlex, Xorosoft, Lido, IntelliChief: all sell AI order entry to distributors in 2026. ([Ordermatic](https://ordermatic.co/), [Elentaria vs Conexiom](https://elentaria.ai/compare/elentaria-vs-conexiom), [StackCube guide](https://blog.stackcube.io/ai-order-entry-software-for-distributors), [ChannelFlex](https://channelflex.com/blog/sales-order-automation-for-distributors-and-manufacturers/), [Xorosoft](https://xorosoft.com/sales-order-automation-distributors/), [Lido list](https://www.lido.app/blog/best-sales-order-automation-software))

**The contrarian case against it:** this is the hottest AI category for distributors right now. Funded products with self-serve sign-up will win most Business Central and QuickBooks buyers within 12 to 18 months. If you sell a £600 a month retainer today, expect the client to ask in year two why they are not on a 300 USD tool.

**Where it still survives:** ERPs these products do not connect to well (Sage 200, Exact Online, Unleashed, Cin7, Katana, Nordic and Finnish systems, or old on-premise ERPs with a database you can reach), and customers with messy part number mapping that needs setup by hand. Only take those jobs.

**Build notes.** Same intake as E1. LLM extracts header and lines. Plain code maps customer part numbers through a mapping table (build it from the last 6 months of orders). Price check against the price list. Create a **draft** sales order (not released) and put exceptions on the approval screen. The processor approves and the ERP sends the confirmation as it does today.

**g. Realistic price.**
* Build: £2,000 to £3,500 (about 2,700 to 4,700 USD).
* Monthly: £400 to £800 (about 530 to 1,070 USD) for a medium firm at 50+ orders a day.
* Aim for payback within 3 months of labour saved, because a product will undercut you later.

### E3. Supplier order confirmation check (upsell only, do not lead with it)

**What it is.** A buyer sends a purchase order. The supplier sends back an order confirmation (PDF) with changed prices, quantities or dates. Someone must compare it to the purchase order in the ERP and flag changes before goods arrive. Both sides agree this is where later chasing and stock problems start. ([Leverage on expediting](https://tryleverage.ai/blog/pf/reduce-supplier-expediting-time), [Learn How To Source](https://blog.learnhowtosource.com/purchase-order-acknowledgment/))

**Size:** medium manufacturers and wholesalers with 30+ suppliers who do not use EDI.

**Existing tools:** Conexiom has a vendor acknowledgement module, and EDI networks like SPS Commerce and TradeCentric cover large suppliers. ([Conexiom solutions](https://conexiom.com/solutions), [SPS Commerce](https://www.spscommerce.com/blog/purchase-order-acknowledgement-poa-spsa/), [TradeCentric](https://tradecentric.com/solution-type/purchase-order-acknowledgement-solution/)) The gap is small firms whose suppliers just email PDFs.

**Why only an upsell:** I found less direct evidence of spend (few job ads name it as a duty), and the owner is often a busy buyer, not a clerk with a clear task. Once you are inside a client for E1 or E2, it is a cheap second workflow using the same intake and matching code. Price: £1,200 to £2,000 build, £150 to £300 a month.

## Step 3: India

**The honest frame first.** Indian SMB software is priced very low: Suvit/Vyapar TaxOne from ₹899 a month, Takkada ₹2,900 to ₹8,500 a year. A data entry operator in Ludhiana earns roughly ₹10,000 to ₹23,000 a month, and a Tally accountant ₹15,000 to ₹45,000 depending on experience. ([Suvit](https://www.suvit.io/pricing), [Takkada](https://takkada.com/), [Glassdoor Ludhiana data entry](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Job/ludhiana-data-entry-jobs-SRCH_IL.0,8_IC2939907_KO9,19.htm), [Apna Ludhiana data entry](https://apna.co/jobs/data_entry-jobs-in-ludhiana), [Jooble Ludhiana Tally accountant](https://in.jooble.org/jobs-tally-accountant/Ludhiana))

So in India, you will not win on "we replace a salary." A monthly fee above ₹8,000 is a hard sell for a small firm. You win only where **errors or speed cost more than a salary**, and where the work mirrors what you will sell in Europe.

### I1. Party ledger reconciliation in Tally (TOP INDIA PICK, mirror of E1)

**a. Who has it, and size.** Traders, distributors, manufacturers and their CA firms. **Small and medium.** For CA firms, the peak is the audit season before tax audit deadlines, when auditors need party balance confirmations.

**b. Steps today.**
1. Start: a supplier or customer sends their ledger of your account (PDF or Excel) by email or WhatsApp. Or your accountant requests it for audit.
2. The accountant exports the same party's ledger from Tally to Excel.
3. They match line by line in Excel.
4. They explain differences. Indian differences have typical causes: TDS deducted by one side but not booked by the other, GST adjustments, debit or credit notes not booked, payments in transit, and plain typing errors. ([OneFinOps vendor reconciliation guide](https://onefinops.com/blog/vendor-payment-reconciliation-guide), [Terra Insight debtors and creditors guide](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/debtors-creditors-reconciliation-india/), [ClearTax on 26AS mismatches](https://cleartax.in/s/26as-reconciliation))
5. End: they pass correction entries in Tally (after the owner or CA approves) and send a confirmation or a difference list to the party.

**c. Software.** WhatsApp or Gmail on one side, TallyPrime on the other. Tally connects through its XML interface over HTTP (by default on port 9000 of the machine running Tally). **Practical catch:** Tally usually runs on a PC in the client's office, so you need a small connector program on that PC, or the client must use Tally on a cloud server. Plan for this in the first meeting.

**d. How often and how long.** Major parties quarterly, all parties at year end. **(Estimate, measure in discovery): 20 to 60 minutes per party**, similar to the UK 30 minute figure.

**e. Who does it.** The in-house accountant (₹15,000 to ₹45,000 a month in Ludhiana), or article assistants at the CA firm.

**f. Existing tools and the gap.**
* Tally's Auditor edition has an external confirmation report, but it shows only the balance difference, not which lines cause it. ([TallyHelp external confirmation](https://help.tallysolutions.com/article/Tally.ERP9/Auditor_Edition/India/Statutory_Audit/External_Confirmation_third_Party.htm))
* Takkada keeps customer ledgers close to matched by linking UPI payments to bills, but that is the receivable side for distributors only. ([Takkada ledger reconciliation](https://takkada.com/blog/ledger-reconciliation-tally-distributor/))
* Terra Insight and AI Accountant sell reconciliation tools for India. ([Terra Insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/debtors-creditors-reconciliation-india/), [AI Accountant](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/automated-statement-reconciliation-india))
* **Gap:** reading the other party's own PDF ledger and matching it line by line to Tally, with Indian reason codes (TDS, GST, debit note), then drafting the WhatsApp or email reply. **Honest warning:** tools exist here too. Treat India as your test bed, not your profit.

**g. Realistic price.**
* Trader or manufacturer: ₹20,000 to ₹40,000 setup, ₹3,000 to ₹6,000 a month.
* CA firm: ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 per audit season for up to about 500 party ledgers.
* For your first 2 clients: do it at cost or free, **in writing, in exchange for the right to publish real numbers and name the client.**

### I2. Emailed or WhatsApp purchase order to sales order in Tally or Busy (mirror of E2)

**a. Who has it.** Ludhiana manufacturers and industrial distributors (fasteners, hand tools, bicycle parts, machine parts) whose business customers send POs as PDFs, Excel files or photos. **Small and medium.** Ludhiana has about 1.5 lakh registered MSMEs and makes around 92% of India's bicycle parts, so there is no shortage of targets within driving distance. ([SME Communities on Ludhiana](https://smecommunities.com/ludhiana-punjabs-industrial-powerhouse-driving-indias-next-sme-revolution/), [DC MSME Ludhiana profile](https://dcmsme.gov.in/old/dips/Ludhiana.pdf))

**b. Steps today.** PO arrives on email or WhatsApp. A clerk reads it, maps the customer's item names to Tally stock items, checks rates, and creates a sales order in Tally or Busy. Then dispatch uses it.

**c. Software.** Gmail or WhatsApp, and TallyPrime or Busy. For WhatsApp you need the WhatsApp Business Platform (API), not the phone app. Meta's India rates are reported at ₹0.115 per utility message and ₹0.8631 per marketing message, plus GST and provider markup, with changes to free service messages reported from 1 October 2026. ([Whautomate rate card](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india), [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026)) Cheapest path: ask the client to forward orders to one dedicated order number or email.

**d. How often and how long.** 10 to 80 orders a day for a medium unit. **(Estimate, measure in discovery): 5 to 10 minutes per order.**

**e. Who does it.** A clerk or accountant at ₹12,000 to ₹25,000 a month.

**f. Existing tools and gap.** The WhatsApp order tools (Rupyz, HublerX, Marg ERP to ERP) are built for FMCG retailers ordering standard SKUs, and many need the retailer to use an app. A factory customer sending a PDF PO with its own part numbers is not their focus. **Contrarian check:** a Ludhiana owner can hire a second clerk for ₹15,000. This only sells where wrong orders cost real money (wrong item dispatched, rework, freight both ways) or where order volume jumps in season.

**g. Realistic price.** ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 setup, ₹4,000 to ₹8,000 a month.

### I3. Export shipment document cross-check (India only)

**a. Who has it.** Ludhiana and Jalandhar exporters of hand tools, hosiery and bicycle parts. **Small and medium.** Search results for local press put Ludhiana's exports at about ₹10,000 crore at risk from US tariffs, which pushes these firms toward Europe. ([Tribune Ludhiana](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/ludhiana/citys-export-lifeline-under-threat-10k-cr-at-risk-amid-us-tariff-shock/))

**b. Steps today.** The exporter already makes the invoice and packing list (often in Export EMS, ExpoMaster or Excel). Then the customs agent (CHA) sends a shipping bill checklist and the shipping line or forwarder sends a draft bill of lading. The documentation executive checks every field (buyer name, HS code, quantities, weights, value, container numbers) against the invoice, packing list and buyer's PO, then approves or asks for changes.

**c. Software.** Email or WhatsApp, the export document tool or Excel, and Tally for the export invoice.

**d. Frequency and time.** 5 to 40 shipments a month. **(Estimate, measure in discovery): 30 to 60 minutes per shipment.**

**e. Who does it.** An export documentation executive, ₹12,000 to ₹35,000 a month in Ludhiana. ([PlacementIndia Ludhiana listings](https://www.placementindia.com/job-search/export-documentation-executive-jobs-in-ludhiana.htm), [Indeed Ludhiana export jobs](https://in.indeed.com/Export-Import-jobs-in-Ludhiana,-Punjab))

**f. Existing tools and gap.** The tools listed above **generate** documents. None I found **checks the documents other companies send back** against your own. A mistake caught after the bill of lading is issued means amendment fees and delay.

**g. Price.** ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 setup, ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 a month.

**Why it is only third:** it makes you local money and friends, but a European buyer does not care about Indian shipping bills. It does not help your real goal.

## Step 4: Workflows in both regions (your case study bridge)

| India version | Europe version | Same parts you reuse | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 Party ledger reconciliation in Tally | E1 Supplier statement reconciliation in Xero or Business Central | Statement reading, balance check, matching engine, exception screen, draft reply | Ledger connector (Tally XML to Xero or BC API), reason codes (TDS and GST vs VAT and credit notes), reply channel (WhatsApp to Outlook) |
| I2 PO to sales order in Tally or Busy | E2 PO to sales order in Business Central, Sage 200, Exact, Unleashed | PO reading, part number mapping table, price check, approval screen | ERP connector, language on some POs |

**Why I1 to E1 is the best bridge:** the European buyer's question is "does your matching work on real, messy statements?" An Indian case study answers that honestly, because Indian ledgers are messier than UK ones (TDS, GST, handwritten notes, WhatsApp photos). Write the case study with numbers only: "412 party ledgers, 87% of lines matched automatically, 61 differences found worth ₹X, accountant time cut from Y hours to Z hours."

**Capture these numbers from day one** (store them in PostgreSQL, not in memory): statements or orders processed, lines per document, percent matched with no human touch, exceptions per document, minutes of human review per document (time the approval screen), and money value of differences found.

## Step 7: Final top picks and why

**1. Supplier statement reconciliation (Europe, medium firms), proven first as party ledger reconciliation in India.**
* It survives e-invoicing. E-invoices will cut the number of lost invoices, but timing differences, missing credit notes and payment gaps will still exist between two companies' books.
* The core is plain matching code, which Developer A can make reliable. AI only reads the statement, and the "rows must add up to the closing balance" check catches its mistakes.
* It is **read only** on the ledger, so it is easier to get access, safer to run, and faster to build.
* The India version is the same job, so your Indian case study is honest proof.
* Weakness: time savings are small, so you must target firms with many suppliers and sell on coverage and money found.

**2. Emailed PO to draft sales order (Europe, medium distributors on under-served ERPs).**
* The strongest labour case (about £1,500 a month at 60 orders a day).
* Weakness: crowded and getting more crowded. Take it only where the products do not fit, and price for fast payback.

**3. Export document cross-check (India only).** Take it if a Ludhiana exporter will pay. Do not spend sales time on it for Europe.

**What I would not do:** build accounts payable invoice capture, WhatsApp order bots for FMCG, GST reconciliation, or freight quoting. Each one already has a cheap or funded product, and the brief says to drop ideas like that.

## Step 8: The next 90 days, with numbers

1. **Weeks 1 to 2:** Build the matching engine and the exception screen against your own sample data. Get 3 real supplier statements and ledgers from any friendly business (a relative's shop, a local CA). Target: 80% of lines matched with no human touch.
2. **Weeks 2 to 6:** Two Indian pilots, free, with written permission to publish numbers. Best targets: one CA firm near the tax audit season, one Ludhiana manufacturer or distributor with 200+ party ledgers on Tally.
3. **Weeks 5 to 8:** Write one case study page with real numbers and a 3 minute screen recording of the exception screen.
4. **Weeks 6 to 12: European sales, through channels, not cold outreach alone.**
   * Ask Developer A's Finnish client for 3 introductions to finance managers. A warm intro from a paying client is worth more than 300 cold emails.
   * Offer white-label work to UK Xero and QuickBooks bookkeeping firms and to Business Central partners: they already have the clients and the trust, and they are short of builders.
   * Ask every prospect one question: "How many supplier statements do you get a month, and how many do you actually reconcile?" If the answer is under 50 received, move on.
5. **Stop rule:** if 10 discovery calls with UK or EU finance managers produce no paid pilot, switch your lead offer to E2 on one ERP where you found a gap, and reuse the same intake and approval screen.
