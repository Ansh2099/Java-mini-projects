ATTACK 6 [MINOR] It targets "small manufacturers on Sage 200" without mentioning that Sage retired Sage 200 Manufacturing
Where: Short answer point 3 and Part 7 pick 1: "UK distributors and small manufacturers on Sage 200 (Standard first)".
Problem: Sage discontinued the Sage 200 Manufacturing module. It was removed in the 2025 R2 release, all support ended on 31 December 2025, and users were pointed to replacements such as Sicon or Cim200, or moved off Sage (Technology Partners, https://technologypartners.co.uk/sage-200-manufacturing-end-of-life/; Sage IE KB, https://ie-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/view2.jsp?k2dockey=250611142825893). As of September 2026, many UK manufacturers on Sage 200 are in or just past a forced change of platform. A firm in that position is a poor buyer for a new Sage 200 integration, and a firm leaving Sage makes the connector worthless. The discovery checklist asks "Which ERP and edition?" but never asks whether the firm is migrating. Counterexample: a Midlands fastener maker on Sage 200 Professional with the old Manufacturing module signs a build in October 2026, then moves to another ERP in Q1 2027. The connector is thrown away before the case study is written.
