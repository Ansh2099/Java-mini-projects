ATTACK 1 [FATAL] The number one pick and the month one plan need a paid Sage developer licence that the 50 USD budget cannot cover
Where: Short answer point 3 ("sell it first to distributors on Sage 200 ... it has a documented sales order API"); "What to do this month" step 2 ("Developer A: build the Tally XML connector and the Sage 200 connector") and step 3 ("Get a Sage 200 test setup through Sage's developer programme or a friendly Sage partner before you pay for anything").
Problem: The task says "Our budget is under 50 USD a month until we have revenue." Sage's own knowledge base says development with the Sage 200 API requires membership of the Sage 200 API Developer Programme, and a search of Sage's UKI Developer Programme pricing returns a fee of about 3,125 GBP a year (or 260 GBP a month), with a further price rise from 1 August 2025 ([Sage KB: Sage 200 API FAQ](https://gb-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/viewsolution.jsp?solutionid=200427112454709), [Sage 50/200 UKI Developer Program pricing](https://downloads.sage.co.uk/download/?did=cd53b8a9-506f-4d67-8ef7-4a016d7a8197), [Sage KB: programme renewal and price updates](https://ie-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/view2.jsp?k2dockey=250725123504990)). Sage also says a test site outside the programme is "subject to our monthly hosting charge". That is about 6 to 7 times the whole monthly budget. The solution says nothing about this cost, and it presents the developer programme as a free way to get a test setup "before you pay for anything". It also tells Developer A to build the Sage 200 connector this month, before any UK client exists. That is the exact point where the budget rule applies. The "friendly Sage partner" option has no name, no link and no terms. So the lead European product cannot be built as the solution describes, and the R5 claim ("3 to 4 weeks on one ERP") assumes access the team does not have.

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] Pick 3 costs the buyer more than it saves, using the solution's own numbers

At the top price and bottom saving, the buyer pays about 18,800 GBP to save 6,800 GBP. Even in year two, 1,100 USD a month is about 10,150 GBP a year against 11,300 GBP saved, which is roughly break even. The solution does the ROI check only for workflow 1 ("The buyer gets back about three times what they pay") and never does it for this one. So a pick is recommended at a price its own evidence says will not sell.

ATTACK 6 [MINOR] The Tally gap ignores Tally's own Excel voucher import
Where: India workflow 1, f.5 ("Docs by Ira ... does not watch the inbox, read Excel files ...") and the buyer filter "PDF or Excel POs".
Problem: Since Release 4.0, TallyPrime has imported Sales and other vouchers from Excel with saved column mapping templates ([TallyHelp: Release 4.0 notes](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/release-notes-tally-prime/release-notes-tallyprime-4/), [TallyHelp: Import Data FAQ](https://help.tallysolutions.com/import-data-faq/)). The solution tells the reader that Excel POs are a gap built in Tally tools cannot handle, and it uses "Excel" to pick the case study client. A prospect whose customers send steady Excel layouts can already use a mapping template. The real gap is only customer item codes and changing layouts. The filter for which prospects to send to Tally's own tools is wrong, and a prospect or their Tally partner can point this out on the first call.
